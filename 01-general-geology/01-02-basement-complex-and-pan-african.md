# 1.3–1.4 — The Basement Complex & the Pan-African Orogeny

## The Basement Complex (Domain 1)

The **Basement Complex** is the ancient crystalline foundation of Nigeria — a mix of
**metamorphic** and **igneous** rocks of **Precambrian** age (Archean to
Proterozoic, roughly 3.6 billion to ~600 million years old). It underlies about
**half the country**: the **northern, central, eastern and western** regions.

### What it's made of

| Rock type | What it is | Where you'll see it |
|-----------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Migmatite** | Mixed igneous/metamorphic "mixed rock" | Widespread in the core |
| **Gneiss** (banded, augen) | High-grade metamorphic, banded | Common |
| **Schist** (mica, quartz, talc) | Medium-grade, flaky/foliated | **Schist belts** (SW) |
| **Quartzite** | Metamorphosed sandstone | Schist belts, ridges |
| **Amphibolite** | Metamorphosed basalt/gabbro | Along belts |
| **Older Granites / granitoids** | Intrusive granites | Throughout |
| **Charnockite** | Hypersthene granite (dry granulite) | Northern massifs |
| **Pegmatite** | Very coarse granite veins | Around granites (ore-bearing!) |
| **Marble / calc-silicate** | Metamorphosed limestone | Local (e.g. Igbeti, Jakura) |

### The terranes (how it's organized)

Geologists group the Basement into broad **terranes** that hint at what you'll find:

- **Migmatite–gneiss core** — deeply metamorphosed, high-grade; the "backbone."
- **Schist belts** — narrow metavolcanic–metasedimentary strips → **gold**.
- **Older Granite plutons** — granitoids intruded during the Pan-African; carry
  **pegmatites** (tin, columbite, gems).
- **Charnockite massifs** — dry, high-grade granulites, mostly in the north.
- **Quartzite ridges & amphibolite** — resistant markers of deformed supracrustals.

### The schist belts — where gold hides

Narrow, elongated **schist belts** (metavolcanic–metasedimentary greenstone-style
sequences) cut through the basement and are Nigeria's prime **gold** ground.
Classic belts include:

- **Ilesa–Ife / Ilesha schist belt** (Osun, Oyo) — the most productive gold belt.
- **Egbe–Isanlu schist belt** (Kogi, Kwara).
- **Igarra schist belt** (Edo).
- **Maru / Anka schist belts** (Zamfara, Katsina, Kebbi) — major artisanal gold.

## The Pan-African Orogeny (~600 Ma)

Near the end of the Precambrian, a major mountain-building event — the
**Pan-African Orogeny** (roughly 750–450 Ma, peaking ~600 Ma) — shook the region
as continents collided (the assembly of **Gondwana**). In Nigeria it:

- **Deformed and metamorphosed** the older basement (folding, foliation).
- **Emplaced granitoids** (Older Granites) and **pegmatites**.
- Created the **structural grain** (lineaments, shear zones) that later fluids
  would follow to deposit ores.

### What the orogeny left behind (and why it matters)

| Legacy | Exploration significance |
|--------|--------------------------|
| Folded, foliated basement | Structures localize fluids → gold in shear zones |
| Older Granite plutons | Pegmatite fields → tin, columbite, gems, lithium |
| NE–SW lineaments/shears | Regional ore "plumbing"; map them from imagery |
| Metamorphosed sediments | Marble, quartzite, kyanite, sillimanite, graphite |

> **Field note.** Much of the "Basement Complex" you walk on is Precambrian rock
> **reworked** by this event — so old ages and overprinting structures coexist.

## States dominated by the Basement Complex

Representative: **Plateau, Kaduna, Kano, Katsina, Zamfara, Kebbi, Niger, Kwara,
Kogi, Nasarawa, FCT (Abuja), Oyo, Osun, Ekiti, Ondo, Edo, Enugu, Ebonyi, Anambra**
(basement inliers), and more. Cross-check the map in **§1.12**.

## Why it matters for exploration

The Basement hosts:

- **Gold** in the schist belts (lode + placer).
- **Tin, columbite, tantalum** in pegmatites around granites (enhanced by the
  later Younger Granites).
- **Gemstones** in pegmatites (emerald, aquamarine, tourmaline, topaz).
- **Industrial minerals** — **mica, feldspar, quartz, talc, marble, dimension granite**.
- **Water** in fractures.

## Exploration targeting in the Basement (recap)

1. **Gold** → map **shear zones and quartz veins** in the schist belts; pan streams.
2. **Tin/columbite/gems** → map and sample **pegmatites** around granite plutons.
3. **Industrial** (kaolin, clay, granite, talc) → weathered granite and alteration zones.
4. Always account for the **laterite/regolith** — sample soil and the saprolite.

## Facts & tips

- **Fact:** the Basement is among the oldest rock on Earth's surface — parts exceed
  **3 billion years**.
- **Fact:** Nigeria's schist belts are the West African equivalent of the world's
  great **greenstone-gold belts**.
- **Tip:** a rusty, sheared, quartz-veined zone in schist is your #1 gold target —
  assay for **Au + As/Sb**.
- **Tip:** pegmatites with black tourmaline (schorl) or greisen alteration are the
  fertile ones for tin/columbite.

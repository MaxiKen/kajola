# 1.13 — Geology of Nigeria at a Glance

One page to remember. Everything else in this handout hangs off this table.

## The three domains

| Domain | Age | Representative rocks | Headline resources | Representative states |
|--------|-----|----------------------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| **Basement Complex** | Precambrian | Migmatite, gneiss, schist, quartzite, older granite, pegmatite, marble | **Gold**, tin, columbite, gems, mica, feldspar, talc, marble, water | Plateau, Kaduna, Osun, Oyo, Kwara, Niger, Edo, Ekiti, FCT, Zamfara, Kebbi… |
| **Younger Granites** | Jurassic (~200–140 Ma) | Ring-complex granite, syenite, pegmatite, greisen | **Tin (cassiterite), columbite–tantalite, tungsten**, gems | Plateau, Bauchi, Kaduna, Nasarawa |
| **Sedimentary basins** | Cretaceous–Recent | Sandstone, shale, limestone, coal, salt, gypsum, laterite | **Oil & gas**, coal, lead-zinc, salt, limestone, gypsum, bitumen, phosphate | Benue, Enugu, Borno, Rivers, Delta, Bayelsa, Ondo, Sokoto… |

## Quick mineral → states mini-reference

- **Gold** — Zamfara, Osun, Kebbi, Oyo, Kwara, Niger, Kogi, Katsina, Edo, Ebonyi.
- **Tin/columbite** — Plateau, Bauchi, Kaduna, Nasarawa, Kano.
- **Coal** — Enugu, Kogi, Nasarawa, Anambra, Benue, Gombe.
- **Lead-zinc** — Ebonyi, Enugu, Plateau, Benue, Cross River, Taraba.
- **Iron ore** — Kogi (Itakpe), Enugu, Nasarawa.
- **Limestone/cement** — Ogun, Enugu, Benue, Kogi, Cross River, Sokoto, Borno.
- **Gypsum** — Gombe, Nasarawa, Bauchi, Borno, Sokoto, Yobe.
- **Bitumen** — Ondo (major), Edo, Lagos, Ogun.
- **Phosphate** — Sokoto, Ogun.
- **Kaolin/clay** — Ondo, Ekiti, Ogun, Edo, Delta, Oyo, Kwara, Kogi (and many more).

## Key numbers to remember

- **Area:** ~923,800 km² · **~half** is Basement Complex.
- **Younger Granites:** **40+** ring complexes, Jurassic (~200–140 Ma).
- **Benue Trough:** ~**800 km** long, NE–SW.
- **Niger Delta:** Africa's largest oil province.
- **Jos Plateau:** a world-class historic **tin** province.

## The one rule that pays off

> **Crystalline basement** (hard granite/gneiss/schist) → think **gold, tin,
> columbite, gems**. **Sedimentary basin** (flat layered rock) → think **oil, gas,
> coal, salt, limestone, lead-zinc**.

# Barite (Baryte)

> **Group:** Industrial/metallic (sulfate) · **Formula:** BaSO₄ · **Element:** Barium (Ba)

## Chemistry & mineralogy
**Barium sulfate** — BaSO₄. A dense, pale mineral that is easy to recognize by its
unusual weight for a light-colored mineral.

## Crystal habit
**Tabular** crystals, rosette "desert roses," fibrous, or **massive/granular**.

## Physical properties & how to test
- **Hardness 3–3.5** (scratched by steel).
- **White to pale** (also yellow, blue, grey); vitreous to pearly.
- **Perfect cleavage** in one direction (flat plates).
- **Very heavy for a pale mineral** — specific gravity ~4.5.
- **Does not fizz** with acid (unlike calcite).

## Genesis
**Hydrothermal** — deposited from barium-rich fluids in veins and fissures, commonly
associated with lead–zinc mineralization.

## States / localities
The **Benue Trough** and adjacent areas: **Nasarawa, Plateau, Benue, Taraba, Cross
River, Kaduna**, and parts of the Basement.

## Mining, uses & market
The main use is as **weighting agent in oil/gas drilling mud**; also a filler in
paint, rubber, and plastics, and in chemicals and **radiation shielding**. Demand
tracks drilling activity.

## Exploration notes
- Look for **heavy, pale, platy** mineral in **veins**, especially near **lead–zinc**
  ground.
- The **high specific gravity** (feels surprisingly heavy) is the key field clue.
- Often a **by-product** of Pb–Zn exploration.

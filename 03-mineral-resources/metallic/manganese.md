# Manganese Ores

> **Group:** Metallic (oxide/hydroxide) · **Minerals:** Pyrolusite (MnO₂), psilomelane · **Element:** Manganese (Mn)

## Chemistry & mineralogy
Chiefly **pyrolusite** (MnO₂) and related oxides/hydroxides (psilomelane,
manganite, braunite). Often mixed with iron oxides in the field.

## Crystal habit
**Pyrolusite:** prismatic/acicular sprays, dendritic, or massive. **Psilomelane:**
botryoidal (grape-like) or stalactitic.

## Physical properties & how to test
- **Hardness:** variable (pyrolusite ~2–6; soils the fingers when soft).
- **Color:** black to dark steel-grey; **streak black**.
- **Luster:** metallic to dull; often **dendritic** (tree-like) coatings.
- Heavy; black streak distinguishes it from look-alikes.

## Genesis
**Sedimentary, residual (lateritic), and hydrothermal** — Mn oxides concentrate in
weathered zones, veins, and sedimentary beds.

## States / localities
Occurrences in the **Basement and schist belts**: **Kebbi, Katsina, Zamfara, Kaduna,
Nasarawa, Plateau, Oyo, Kwara**, and elsewhere. Nigeria's manganese is mostly
small-to-medium scale.

## Mining, uses & market
Essential in **steelmaking** (deoxidizer/desulfurizer), **batteries** (dry-cell,
Li-ion cathodes), **alloys**, **fertilizers**, and **pigments**.

## Exploration notes
- **Black, streak-black, dendritic or botryoidal** oxides in veins/regolith.
- **Soil/rock geochemistry** for **Mn (+ Fe, Co, Ba)**.
- Distinguish from **hematite/limonite** by the **black streak** (hematite is red-brown).

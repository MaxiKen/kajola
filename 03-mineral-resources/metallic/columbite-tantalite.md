# Columbite–Tantalite (Coltan)

> **Group:** Metallic · **Formula:** (Fe,Mn)(Nb,Ta)₂O₆ · **Elements:** Niobium (Nb), Tantalum (Ta)

## Chemistry & mineralogy
A **solid-solution series**: **columbite** (Nb-rich) → **tantalite** (Ta-rich).
Together marketed as **"coltan."** An oxide of niobium and tantalum.

## Crystal habit
**Orthorhombic** — short, stubby prismatic crystals, often in parallel groups, or
**massive/granular**.

## Physical properties & how to test
- **Hardness 6–6.5.**
- **Luster:** submetallic; **color** iron-black to brownish-black.
- **Streak** black to dark grey.
- **Heavy** — specific gravity rises with Ta content (~5–8).
- Often occurs with **cassiterite** in the same rock.

## Genesis
Concentrated by **magmatic and hydrothermal processes** in **rare-element
pegmatites** — typically in the zoned interiors of pegmatites, alongside cassiterite,
tourmaline, and lithium minerals.

## States / localities
**Jos Plateau** (**Plateau**), plus pegmatite fields in **Nasarawa, Kaduna, Bauchi,
Kogi, Oyo**.

## Mining, uses & market
- **Niobium** — high-strength steel alloys, superalloys, jet engines.
- **Tantalum** — compact, high-performance **capacitors** for electronics (phones,
  laptops); a strategically important and price-volatile "conflict mineral."

## Exploration notes
- Map and sample **zoned pegmatites** around the **Younger Granites**.
- **Heavy-mineral/panning** and **radiometrics** (columbite is often associated with
  U/Th minerals) are effective tools.
- Black, heavy grains with cassiterite are the classic association.

# Chromite

> **Group:** Metallic (oxide, spinel group) · **Formula:** FeCr₂O₄ · **Element:** Chromium (Cr)

## Chemistry & mineralogy
**Chromium iron oxide** — FeCr₂O₄, a member of the **spinel** group. The only
economically important **ore of chromium**.

## Crystal habit
Usually **massive or granular**; rare octahedral crystals. Occurs as disseminated
grains or concentrated layers in ultramafic rock.

## Physical properties & how to test
- **Hardness 5.5.**
- **Color:** black to brownish-black; submetallic.
- **Streak:** brown.
- **Heavy**; weakly magnetic.
- Distinguished from magnetite by its **brown streak** and lack of strong magnetism.

## Genesis
**Magmatic concentration** of chromium in ultramafic (mantle-derived) rocks, often as
layers or pods.

## States / localities
Minor in Nigeria — occurs in **ultramafic/serpentinite bodies** within some Basement
and schist-belt settings (e.g. parts of the southwest and central basement). Not a
major producer, but worth recognizing.

## Mining, uses & market
Smelted to **ferrochrome** for **stainless steel** and superalloys; also refractories
and chrome chemicals. Chromium is a strategic metal.

## Exploration notes
- Search **serpentinite/ultramafic bodies** for dense black chromite grains.
- **Geochemistry (Cr, Ni, Co)** and heavy-mineral studies are effective.
- Field clue: **brown-streaked black grains** in greenish serpentinite.

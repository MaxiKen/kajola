# Titanium Minerals (Ilmenite, Rutile, Leucoxene)

> **Group:** Metallic (oxide) · **Minerals:** Ilmenite FeTiO₃ · Rutile TiO₂ · **Element:** Titanium (Ti)

## Chemistry & mineralogy
- **Ilmenite** — iron titanate, FeTiO₃ (the main Ti ore).
- **Rutile** — titanium dioxide, TiO₂ (higher Ti content).
- **Leucoxene** — fine-grained, altered ilmenite (enriched in TiO₂).

## Crystal habit
**Ilmenite:** massive, platy, or sand-sized grains. **Rutile:** prismatic, acicular,
or knee-twinned crystals (also sand grains).

## Physical properties & how to test
- **Ilmenite:** hardness 5–6; black, metallic; **black streak**; heavy; weakly magnetic.
- **Rutile:** hardness 6–6.5; reddish-brown to black, adamantine; **pale yellow-brown
  streak**; heavy.

## Genesis
**Placer concentration** — dense Ti minerals are sorted and concentrated by waves and
rivers from source rocks.

## States / localities
Coastal and river heavy-mineral sands: **Lagos, Ondo, Delta, Rivers, Akwa Ibom,
Cross River**, and river placers inland.

## Mining, uses & market
Source of **titanium dioxide pigment** (paints, paper, plastics) and **titanium metal**
(strong, light — aerospace, medical implants, welding electrodes).

## Exploration notes
- **Sample beach/river sands** and pan for dense black grains.
- **Black sands** (ilmenite + magnetite) are the target horizon.
- Separate ilmenite (black streak) from rutile (pale streak) and magnetite (magnetic).

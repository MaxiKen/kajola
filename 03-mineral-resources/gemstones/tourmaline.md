# Tourmaline

> **Group:** Gemstone · **Mineral:** tourmaline (complex borosilicate)

## What it is
**Tourmaline** — a complex boron silicate that occurs in **many colors**:
- **Schorl** — black (the most common, and a useful indicator in pegmatites).
- **Elbaite** group — green (*verdelite*), pink/red (*rubellite*), blue (*indicolite*),
  and bicolored "**watermelon**" tourmaline (the gem prize).

## Properties & how to recognize
- **Hardness 7–7.5**; **striated, triangular/prismatic** crystals (a key ID feature).
- **Piezoelectric** (generates charge under pressure) and pyroelectric.
- Vitreous luster; transparent to opaque (black schorl is opaque).

## Where in Nigeria (states)
**Jos Plateau (Plateau)** pegmatites, plus **Nasarawa, Oyo, and Kaduna**.

## Uses & value
- **Gemstone** — vividly colored elbaite (rubellite, indicolite, watermelon) is prized.
- **Industrial** — piezoelectric applications.

## Exploration / notes
- The **striated triangular prism** is unmistakable.
- **Black schorl** flags fertile pegmatites; look nearby for gemmy elbaite, beryl, and
  columbite.

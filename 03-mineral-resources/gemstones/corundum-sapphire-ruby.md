# Corundum — Sapphire & Ruby

> **Group:** Gemstone / abrasive · **Formula:** Al₂O₃

## What it is
**Corundum** — aluminium oxide. Its gem varieties are among the most valuable:
- **Ruby** — red (colored by chromium).
- **Sapphire** — blue (iron + titanium); all **non-red** colors are "fancy sapphires"
  (yellow, pink, green, etc.).

## Properties & how to recognize
- **Hardness 9** — the second-hardest mineral (after diamond); scratches almost
  everything.
- Adamantine to vitreous luster; no cleavage (tough).
- Heavy; often in rounded alluvial grains.

## Where in Nigeria (states)
Sapphire is found in parts of **Kaduna** and **Oyo** (including the Ibadan area), and in
**alluvial (placer)** deposits; ruby is rare in Nigeria.

## Uses & value
- **Gemstones** — ruby and sapphire are "precious" gems of the highest value.
- **Abrasive** (emery) and synthetic **scratch-resistant watch crystals**.

## Exploration / notes
- **Hardness 9** is the giveaway (scratches a knife/steel easily; not scratched itself).
- In Nigeria, look in **metamorphic rocks and alluvial gravels** (see
  [Placers & alluvium](../../02-rocks-of-nigeria/sedimentary/placers-alluvium.md)).

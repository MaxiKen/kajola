# Emerald

> **Group:** Gemstone · **Mineral:** beryl (green variety) · **Formula:** Be₃Al₂Si₆O₁₈

## What it is
**Emerald** — the **green variety of beryl**, colored by traces of **chromium and/or
vanadium**. One of the four "precious" gemstones (with diamond, ruby, sapphire) and
one of Nigeria's best-known gems.

## Properties & how to recognize
- **Hardness 7.5–8.**
- **Green** (the prized color); **hexagonal prismatic** crystals.
- Usually has **inclusions** (called the "jardin") — flawless emeralds are very rare.
- Vitreous luster; translucent.

## Where in Nigeria (states)
The **Jos Plateau (Plateau State)** is Nigeria's emerald heartland; also reported in
parts of **Kaduna** and **Oyo**. Nigerian emeralds are mined artisanally and sold
internationally.

## Uses & value
A **high-value gemstone** for fine jewelry. Value is driven by **color** (vivid green),
**clarity**, and **size** — top stones rival the world's best.

## Exploration / notes
- Look in **pegmatites and mica schist** (see [Pegmatite](../../02-rocks-of-nigeria/igneous/pegmatite.md),
  [Mica schist](../../02-rocks-of-nigeria/metamorphic/mica-schist-phyllite.md)).
- Green beryl crystals in matrix are the target; often with **aquamarine** and
  **tourmaline**.

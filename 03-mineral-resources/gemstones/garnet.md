# Garnet

> **Group:** Gemstone / abrasive · **Mineral:** garnet group (silicates)

## What it is
The **garnet group** — a family of silicate minerals with several gem/industrial
species: **almandine** (red), **pyrope** (red), **spessartine** (orange),
**grossular** (incl. brown-orange "hessonite" and green "tsavorite"), and **andradite**
(incl. green demantoid).

## Properties & how to recognize
- **Hardness 6.5–7.5**; typically **resinous** luster.
- Often forms **dodecahedral** (12-sided) or rounded crystals.
- **Isotropic** (behaves the same in all directions optically); no cleavage.
- Commonly **red to orange-brown**, but many colors exist.

## Where in Nigeria (states)
In **metamorphic rocks** — schist, marble, and skarn — across the Basement:
**Jos (Plateau), Kaduna, Oyo, Kwara, Kogi**, and elsewhere.

## Uses & value
- **Gemstone** (almandine, pyrope, spessartine, grossular, demantoid).
- **Abrasive** — garnet sandpaper/blasting media and **water-jet cutting**.

## Exploration / notes
- Look in **schist, marble, and contact (skarn) zones** (see
  [Mica schist](../../02-rocks-of-nigeria/metamorphic/mica-schist-phyllite.md),
  [Marble](../../02-rocks-of-nigeria/metamorphic/marble-calc-silicate.md)).
- Rounded 12-sided crystals + resinous luster are the giveaway.

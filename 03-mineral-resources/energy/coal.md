# Coal & Lignite

> **Group:** Energy · **Material:** coal (solid fossil fuel)

## What it is
**Coal** — a combustible sedimentary rock formed from compacted plant material
(peat → lignite → bituminous coal → anthracite). A traditional fuel and chemical
feedstock.

## Where in Nigeria (states)
- **Enugu** — the historic "Coal City," Nigeria's main coal-mining center.
- **Kogi** (Ogboyaga/Ogboyega), **Nasarawa** (Lafia/Obi), **Benue, Anambra**.
- **Lignite** in the **Chad Basin (Borno)** and southwestern basins.

## Uses & value
- **Power generation** and **industry**; historically important at Enugu.
- Source of **coke, tar, and chemicals**; potential **coal-bed methane**.

## Exploration / notes
- Coal occurs in the **"coal measures"** (sandstone/shale sequences) of the Benue Trough
  (see [Coal (rock)](../../02-rocks-of-nigeria/sedimentary/coal.md)).
- Assess **seam thickness, depth, rank, and sulfur/ash** content for viability.

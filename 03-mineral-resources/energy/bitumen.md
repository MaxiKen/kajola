# Bitumen (Tar Sands)

> **Group:** Energy · **Material:** natural asphalt (heavy hydrocarbon)

## What it is
**Bitumen** — a thick, sticky, black **heavy hydrocarbon** that coats sand grains in
"tar sands" or "oil sands." A very viscous form of petroleum and a potential
**unconventional oil resource**.

## Where in Nigeria (states)
- **Ondo State** — very large bitumen/tar-sand deposits (Akoko/Ikale area), among the
  biggest in the region.
- **Edo, Lagos, Ogun** — the southwestern (Dahomey Basin) bitumen belt.

## Uses & value
- **Road surfacing / asphalt** — the immediate use.
- A significant **potential unconventional oil resource** — Ondo's deposits could yield
  large volumes of heavy oil if developed.

## Exploration / notes
- **Black, sticky, petroleum-smelling** sand (see [Bitumen (rock)](../../02-rocks-of-nigeria/sedimentary/bitumen.md)).
- Development depends on **recovery technology and oil price** (heavy oil is costlier to
  extract).

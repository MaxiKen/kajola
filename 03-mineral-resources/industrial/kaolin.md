# Kaolin (China Clay)

> **Group:** Industrial / non-metallic · **Mineral:** kaolinite

## What it is
A **white clay** dominated by the mineral **kaolinite**, formed by the intense
weathering of feldspar-rich rocks (granites). Nigeria's most valuable clay resource.

## Properties & how to recognize
- **White** (high-grade) to cream; **soft, soapy, and earthy**.
- **Plastic when wet**, hard and porous when fired.
- Fine and smooth; low iron content (whiteness = quality).

## Where in Nigeria (states)
Formed by deep tropical weathering across the Basement: **Ondo, Ekiti, Ogun, Edo,
Delta, Oyo, Osun, Kwara, Kogi, Enugu**, and more.

## Uses
- **CERAMICS** (porcelain, tiles), **paper coating**, **paint, rubber, plastics**,
  **refractories**, and **cosmetics**.

## Exploration / notes
- Assess **whiteness, plasticity, and iron content** (lower iron = higher value).
- Often associated with **laterite profiles** (see [Laterite](../../02-rocks-of-nigeria/sedimentary/laterite-ferricrete.md)).
- Distinguished from common brick clay by its **white color and fine, soapy feel**.

# Diatomite (Diatomaceous Earth)

> **Group:** Industrial / non-metallic · **Material:** biogenic silica (opal-A) from diatoms
> **Quick ID:** Very **light and porous** — feels chalky and floats readily; white to pale tan; **absorbs water** like a sponge; soots the fingers.

## At a glance

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Colour | White to pale tan/cream |
| Density | **Very light** (floats readily) |
| Feel | Chalky; soots the fingers |
| Absorption | Very high (absorbs like a sponge) |
| Key test | Extreme lightness + chalky + floats |

## What it is
**Diatomite** — a soft, lightweight rock made of the fossilized silica shells of **diatoms** (microscopic algae). Also called **diatomaceous earth**. It is essentially fossilized algae skeletons.

## Chemistry & mineralogy
**Biogenic silica** — amorphous opaline silica (opal-A) from the frustules (shells) of **diatoms**. It is hydrous SiO₂, very porous and lightweight because of the intricate shell structure.

## Crystal habit
**Earthy / porous** — diatomite is a mass of microscopic diatom shells; identified by its extreme lightness and chalky feel, not visible crystals.

## Physical properties
- **Very light and porous** — feels chalky and floats readily.
- White to pale tan/cream; **absorbs water** like a sponge.
- Soils the fingers (fine silica); very low density.

## How to identify in the field
1. **Weight:** remarkably **light** — floats on water.
2. **Feel:** chalky, porous.
3. **Absorption:** absorbs water like a sponge.
4. **Colour:** white to pale tan/cream.
5. **Context:** lake/lagoon sediments.

## Lookalikes & how to distinguish

| Looks like | How to tell it apart |
|---|---|
| **Chalk** | Chalk **fizzes** with acid; diatomite does not. |
| **Kaolin** | Kaolin is heavier and not as chalky/light; diatomite is extremely light. |
| **Pumice** | Pumice is volcanic glass and glassy; diatomite is chalky and biogenic. |

## Genesis
**Biogenic sedimentary** — accumulates from the shells of **diatoms** in lakes and lagoons. Diatoms (algae) build silica shells that settle and accumulate as diatomite in quiet aquatic settings.

## States / localities
Forms in **lakes and lagoons**; reported in the **Chad Basin (Borno)** and other lacustrine settings.

## Associated minerals & host rocks
Diatom shells (opal-A), volcanic ash, clay; hosted by **lacustrine/lagoonal sediments** (see [Clay (brick/pottery)](clay.md)).

## Mining, uses & market
- **Filtration** (water, beer, swimming pools), **insulation**, and **absorbents**.
- Mild **abrasive** (polishes, toothpaste), **filler**, and **insecticide**.
- Valued for its high porosity and low density.

## Exploration notes
- Its **extreme lightness and chalky feel** are unmistakable.
- Assess **purity and porosity** for filtration uses.
- Target lake/lagoon sedimentary sequences (e.g. the Chad Basin).

## Facts
- Diatomite is made of **fossilized diatom** (algae) shells.
- It is **so light it floats** on water — an unmistakable test.
- It is used for **filtration** (beer, pools, water) thanks to its porosity.
- It is also a mild **abrasive** and natural **insecticide**.

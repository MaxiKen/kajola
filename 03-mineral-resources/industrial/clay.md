# Clay (Brick & Pottery)

> **Group:** Industrial / non-metallic · **Minerals:** mixed clay minerals

## What it is
Common **clay** — a mix of clay minerals (kaolinite, illite, smectite), silt, and
sometimes iron oxides. Less pure than kaolin, but abundant and essential for bricks
and pottery.

## Properties & how to recognize
- **Plastic and sticky when wet**; shrinks and hardens when dried and **fired**.
- Color ranges from **grey/yellow to red/brown** (iron content).
- Earthy texture; may smell "clayey" when damp.

## Where in Nigeria (states)
Widespread — suitable clay underlies **most states**, supporting a huge brick,
tile, and pottery industry nationwide.

## Uses
- **Bricks, tiles, blocks, and pottery** — the backbone of local construction.
- **Cement** raw material; **earthenware**.

## Exploration / notes
- Test **plasticity** and **firing behavior** (shrinkage, color after firing).
- Red clays are iron-rich; paler clays are better for finer ceramics.
- Distinguished from **kaolin** by color (usually not white) and higher impurities.

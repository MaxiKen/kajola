# Quartz, Silica & Glass Sand

> **Group:** Industrial / non-metallic · **Formula:** SiO₂

## What it is
**Quartz** (silicon dioxide, SiO₂) — pure **silica**. As **glass sand** (high-purity
quartz sand) it is the essential raw material for making glass.

## Properties & how to recognize
- **Hardness 7** — scratches glass and steel.
- **Glassy** luster; white/clear/grey; **no cleavage** (conchoidal fracture).
- **Does not fizz** with acid; chemically resistant.

Sourced from **quartz veins, quartzite, and pure quartz sands** (beaches, dunes, rivers).

## Where in Nigeria (states)
Quartz from **granites, pegmatites, and quartzite** across the Basement; **glass-grade
sand** from coastal/river sands — **Lagos, Ondo, Delta, Ogun**, and elsewhere.

## Uses
- **GLASS** manufacture (the main use of glass sand).
- **Foundry** sand, **silicon metal**, **abrasives**, **filters**, and **fillers**.

## Exploration / notes
- For **glass**, purity matters — assess **SiO₂ content and iron** (low iron = clear glass).
- Distinguish from **feldspar** (feldspar has two cleavages; quartz has none).

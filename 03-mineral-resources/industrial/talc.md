# Talc (Soapstone)

> **Group:** Industrial / non-metallic · **Mineral:** talc (Mg silicate)

## What it is
**Talc** — the **softest mineral** on Earth (hardness 1) — a hydrated magnesium
silicate. In massive form it is called **soapstone** or **steatite**.

## Properties & how to recognize
- **Hardness 1** — the softest mineral; easily scratched by a fingernail.
- **Soapy, greasy feel** — hence "soapstone."
- White, grey, or **green**; pearly luster; splits into flexible sheets.

## Where in Nigeria (states)
In altered **ultramafic zones** of the Basement: reported in **Kwara, Oyo, Kaduna,
Plateau**, and some schist belts.

## Uses
- **COSMETICS** (talcum powder), **ceramics, paper, paint, and plastics**.
- **Soapstone** for carving, countertops, and stove liners.
- Industrial lubricant and filler.

## Exploration / notes
- The **soapy feel + extreme softness** is unmistakable.
- Associated with **chlorite/serpentine** alteration of ultramafic rocks (see
  [Talc/Chlorite schist](../../02-rocks-of-nigeria/metamorphic/talc-chlorite-schist.md)).

# Dimension Stone (Building & Ornamental Stone)

> **Group:** Industrial / non-metallic · **Material:** granite, marble, quartzite, sandstone (use category)
> **Quick ID:** Hard, durable, **massive** rock of good colour that takes a polish/finish and yields large blocks; low porosity, weather-resistant.

## At a glance

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Types | Granite, marble, quartzite, sandstone |
| Hardness & durability | Hard, weather-resistant, low porosity |
| Workability | Quarried in large blocks; cut and polished |
| Signature | Sound, unfractured, good colour, high block yield |
| Key test | Massive + durable + good colour + block yield |

## Chemistry & mineralogy
Not a single mineral but a **use category**: natural rock cut to specific sizes for construction and ornament. The main types in Nigeria are **granite, marble, quartzite, and sandstone** — any hard, durable, massive rock.

## Crystal habit / texture
Massive, competent, and durable rock; attractive colour and pattern; takes a polish (granite, marble) or a smooth finish (sandstone, quartzite).

## Physical properties
- **Hardness & durability:** hard, weather-resistant, low porosity.
- **Workability:** can be quarried in large blocks and cut/polished.
- **Signature:** sound, unfractured, good-colour rock with high block yield.

## How to identify in the field
1. **Massiveness:** thick, unfractured, competent rock.
2. **Durability:** hard, weather-resistant, low porosity.
3. **Colour/pattern:** attractive, uniform.
4. **Workability:** yields large blocks; takes a polish or finish.
5. **Joints:** widely spaced (more block yield).

## Lookalikes & how to distinguish

| Looks like | How to tell it apart |
|---|---|
| **Gneiss** | Gneiss is banded/foliated (poor block yield); dimension granite is massive and unbanded. |
| **Weathered/fractured rock** | Fractured, weathered rock has poor block yield — not dimension-grade. |
| **Schist** | Schist splits easily along foliation; dimension stone is massive and unjointed. |

## Genesis
- **Granite** — plutonic igneous (see [Granite](granite.md)).
- **Marble** — metamorphosed limestone (see [Marble](marble.md)).
- **Quartzite / sandstone** — metamorphic / sedimentary (see [Quartzite](../../02-rocks-of-nigeria/metamorphic/quartzite-quartz-schist.md), [Sandstone](../../02-rocks-of-nigeria/sedimentary/sandstone.md)).

## States / localities
**Ogun, Oyo, Kogi, Ebonyi, Ondo, FCT** — major **granite** quarrying (notably around Abuja/FCT); **marble** in Kogi, Oyo, Kwara; quartzite in several Basement areas.

## Associated minerals & host rocks
Depends on type: granite (quartz, feldspar, mica), marble (calcite, dolomite), quartzite (quartz), sandstone (quartz, cement).

## Mining, uses & market
- **Uses:** buildings, **countertops**, tiles, monuments, cladding, paving, kerbstones.
- **Market:** construction-driven; **FCT/Abuja granite** is a major quarrying industry.

## Exploration notes
- Seek **massive, unfractured bodies** with good colour and few defects; assess **block yield** and joint spacing.
- The main types are profiled individually: [Granite](granite.md), [Marble](marble.md), [Quartzite](../../02-rocks-of-nigeria/metamorphic/quartzite-quartz-schist.md).

- [ ] Massive and unfractured?

## Facts
- Dimension stone is a **use category** — any hard, massive, attractive rock.
- **FCT/Abuja granite** is a major Nigerian quarrying industry.
- Value depends on **colour, uniformity, and block yield**.
- Granite and marble take a high **polish**; sandstone/quartzite take a finish.

# Dimension Stone (Building & Ornamental Stone)

## Chemistry & mineralogy
Not a single mineral but a **use category**: natural rock cut to specific sizes for
construction and ornament. The main types in Nigeria are **granite, marble, quartzite,
and sandstone** — any hard, durable, massive rock.

## Crystal habit / texture
Massive, competent, and durable rock; attractive colour and pattern; takes a polish
(granite, marble) or a smooth finish (sandstone, quartzite).

## Physical properties & how to test
- **Hardness & durability:** hard, weather-resistant, low porosity.
- **Workability:** can be quarried in large blocks and cut/polished.
- **Signature:** sound, unfractured, good-colour rock with high block yield.

## Genesis
- **Granite** — plutonic igneous (see [Granite](granite.md)).
- **Marble** — metamorphosed limestone (see [Marble](marble.md)).
- **Quartzite / sandstone** — metamorphic / sedimentary (see [Quartzite](../../02-rocks-of-nigeria/metamorphic/quartzite-quartz-schist.md)).

## States / localities
**Ogun, Oyo, Kogi, Ebonyi, Ondo, FCT** — major **granite** quarrying (notably around
Abuja/FCT); **marble** in Kogi, Oyo, Kwara; quartzite in several Basement areas.

## Mining, uses & market
- **Uses:** buildings, **countertops**, tiles, monuments, cladding, paving, kerbstones.
- **Market:** construction-driven; **FCT/Abuja granite** is a major quarrying industry.

## Exploration notes
- Seek **massive, unfractured bodies** with good colour and few defects; assess **block
  yield** and joint spacing.

# Granite (Dimension & Ornamental Stone)

> **Group:** Industrial / non-metallic · **Material:** granite

## What it is
**Granite** used as **dimension/ornamental stone** — one of Nigeria's biggest quarrying
industries. Hard, durable, and attractive, it is cut into blocks, tiles, and slabs.

## Properties & how to recognize
- **Hard, durable, massive** (no foliation/banding — that would be gneiss).
- Interlocking crystals of quartz (grey), feldspar (pink/white), and mica (black).
- Takes a high **polish**; resists weathering.

## Where in Nigeria (states)
Quarried nationwide: **FCT (Abuja), Ogun, Oyo, Ondo, Ekiti, Osun, Kwara, Plateau,
Kaduna, Kano**, and more.

## Uses
- **Dimension & ornamental stone** — tiles, countertops, monuments, cladding.
- **Construction aggregate**, road metal, and **kerbstones**.

## Exploration / notes
- Assess **color, grain size, uniformity, and durability** (these set the price).
- Distinguish from **gneiss** (banded) and **charnockite** (contains hypersthene).

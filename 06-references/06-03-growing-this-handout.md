## Profile templates (recap)
Every rock and mineral follows the same dense structure so you can compare specimens quickly.

**Rock profile** → Title + quick-ID blockquote · **At-a-glance table** · Composition · Origin & classification · Texture/structures · Diagnostic field features · Identification tests · Setting/formation · Host states/localities · Associated minerals/rocks · Economic relevance · **Lookalikes table** · **Field checklist** · Field tips · **Facts** · images (hand specimen + in situ) · Related.

**Mineral profile** → Title + quick-ID blockquote · **At-a-glance table** · Chemistry & mineralogy · Crystal habit · Physical properties (and how to test) · Step-by-step ID · **Lookalikes table** · Occurrence with images (pure + in rock/ore) · Genesis · States/localities · Associated minerals & host rocks · Mining, uses & market · Exploration notes · **Field checklist** · **Facts** · Related.

*(Part IV provinces use an At-a-glance table + geology/structure/resources/significance/field-tips/facts; Part V field guides use At-a-glance + step-by-step + pitfalls + facts.)*

## Image conventions
- **Real photographs only** — web-sourced specimens, with a **source credit** in the caption. No AI-generated images.
- **Two-image rule** for minerals: a **pure/hand-specimen** image *and* an **in-rock/ore occurrence** image. (For massive igneous rocks and bulk industrial materials, one hand-specimen image is acceptable since pure ≈ in-rock.)
- Store images in `assets/images/` and reference them with a **relative path**.
- Give every image a short caption *with its source*.

## Adding a new rock or mineral
1. Create a file in the right folder (`02-rocks-of-nigeria/…`, `03-mineral-resources/…`).
2. Use the dense profile template above.
3. Add two credited images (minerals) or one (massive/bulk).
4. Link it from the relevant **README** table and from related profiles.
5. Run a **link check** so nothing breaks.

## Citation style
- Inline links to related profiles and the sources listed in **[§6.1](06-01-key-references.md)** and **[§6.2](06-02-online-resources-maps.md)**.
- Always cross-check localities against the current **NGGS** map and **GSN/company** reports before relying on them in the field.

## A closing note
Geology is learned in the **field**, not just on the page. Use this handout to know what to *look for* — then go and look, describe, sample, and record. Keep your own notes, add your photos, and let the handout grow with your experience.

> *"The rock doesn't lie — but it rewards the prepared eye."*

## Facts
- Every rock, mineral, province, and field guide follows a **comparable dense structure**.
- **Real, credited photos only** — never AI-generated images.
- Minerals get **two images** (pure + in-rock/ore); bulk materials get one.
- Always **link-check** after adding an entry so nothing breaks.

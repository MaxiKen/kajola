# 6.2 — Online Resources, Datasets & Geological Maps

Where to find data, maps, and up-to-date information.

## Official (Nigeria)
- **Nigerian Geological Survey Agency (NGGS / GSN)** — geological maps, bulletins, and
  geoscience data; the first stop for authoritative regional geology.
- **Nigerian Mining Cadastre Office (MCO)** — mining titles, licenses, and cadastral data.
- **Federal Ministry of Mines and Steel Development** — policy, sector reports, cadastre.

## Global agencies & databases
- **USGS Minerals Yearbook** — *Nigeria* chapter (annual production/trade):
  `usgs.gov`.
- **USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries** — global context for key commodities.
- **Mindat.org** — the world's largest mineral & locality database (search Nigerian
  localities).
- **British Geological Survey (BGS)** — African geoscience data & some Nigeria datasets.
- **OneGeology** and the **Africa Groundwater Atlas (IGRAC/UNESCO)** — freely viewable
  geological maps (including a Nigeria geological map used in this handout).

## Remote sensing & topography (free)
- **Sentinel-2 / Landsat** imagery (via Copernicus/USGS EarthExplorer) — for
  **lineament and alteration mapping** (see [§5.3](../05-field-identification/05-03-alteration-gossans.md)).
- **SRTM / ASTER DEM** — topography & drainage (useful for placer and access work).

## Maps worth having
- **NGGS 1:2,000,000 Geological Map of Nigeria** — the national overview.
- **1:100,000 geological map sheets** — for detailed fieldwork.
- **Bouguer gravity & aeromagnetic compilations** — for structure and depth-to-basement
  work.

## Literature search
- **Google Scholar, ResearchGate, AAPG Datapages, ScienceDirect, GeoScienceWorld** —
  search by province name (e.g. "Benue Trough", "Niger Delta", "Younger Granites
  Nigeria").

> **Tip:** combine the **NGGS map** (regional geology) with **free satellite imagery**
> (lineaments/alteration) to generate your first exploration targets.

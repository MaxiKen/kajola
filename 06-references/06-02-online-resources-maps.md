# 6.2 — Online Resources, Datasets & Geological Maps

> **Start here:** For authoritative geology → **NGGS/GSN**. For production data → **USGS**. For mineral localities → **Mindat.org**. For targets → combine the **NGGS map** with **free satellite imagery**.
> **Golden rule:** Pair a regional geology map with satellite imagery to generate your first targets.

## At a glance — quick links

| Purpose | Resource |
|---|---|
| Authoritative geology | **NGGS / GSN** (maps, bulletins) |
| Mining titles & cadastre | **Nigerian Mining Cadastre Office (MCO)** |
| Production & trade | **USGS Minerals Yearbook** (Nigeria chapter) |
| Mineral localities | **Mindat.org** |
| Lineament/alteration mapping | **Sentinel-2 / Landsat** (Copernicus / EarthExplorer) |
| Topography | **SRTM / ASTER DEM** |

Where to find data, maps, and up-to-date information.

## Official (Nigeria)
- **Nigerian Geological Survey Agency (NGGS / GSN)** — geological maps, bulletins, and geoscience data; the first stop for authoritative regional geology.
- **Nigerian Mining Cadastre Office (MCO)** — mining titles, licenses, and cadastral data.
- **Federal Ministry of Mines and Steel Development** — policy, sector reports, cadastre.

## Global agencies & databases
- **USGS Minerals Yearbook** — *Nigeria* chapter (annual production/trade): `usgs.gov`.
- **USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries** — global context for key commodities.
- **Mindat.org** — the world's largest mineral & locality database (search Nigerian localities).
- **British Geological Survey (BGS)** — African geoscience data & some Nigeria datasets.
- **OneGeology** and the **Africa Groundwater Atlas (IGRAC/UNESCO)** — freely viewable geological maps (including a Nigeria geological map used in this handout).

## Remote sensing & topography (free)
- **Sentinel-2 / Landsat** imagery (via Copernicus/USGS EarthExplorer) — for **lineament and alteration mapping** (see [§5.3](../05-field-identification/05-03-alteration-gossans.md)).
- **SRTM / ASTER DEM** — topography & drainage (useful for placer and access work).

## Maps worth having
- **NGGS 1:2,000,000 Geological Map of Nigeria** — the national overview.
- **1:100,000 geological map sheets** — for detailed fieldwork.
- **Bouguer gravity & aeromagnetic compilations** — for structure and depth-to-basement work.

## Literature search
- **Google Scholar, ResearchGate, AAPG Datapages, ScienceDirect, GeoScienceWorld** — search by province name (e.g. "Benue Trough", "Niger Delta", "Younger Granites Nigeria").

> **Tip:** combine the **NGGS map** (regional geology) with **free satellite imagery** (lineaments/alteration) to generate your first exploration targets.

## A quick-start target-generation workflow
1. Download the **NGGS regional map** + **Sentinel-2** imagery for your area.
2. Trace **lineaments** (faults/shears) and map **alteration/gossans** from imagery.
3. Overlay on the province geology to pick a **play type** (see [Part IV](../04-geological-provinces/README.md)).
4. Check **Mindat/USGS** for known occurrences, then plan field reconnaissance.

## Facts
- The **NGGS 1:2,000,000** map is the national geological overview.
- **Mindat.org** is the best free mineral-locality database.
- **Sentinel-2/Landsat** imagery is free and ideal for **lineament/alteration** mapping.
- The **MCO** holds official mining-title and cadastral data.

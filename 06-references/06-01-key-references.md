# 6.1 — Key References (NGGS/GSN & Academic Literature)

> **Start here:** For a single-volume synthesis → **Obaje (2009)**. For authoritative regional geology → **NGGS maps & GSN bulletins**. For current production → **USGS**. *Always verify editions, years, and page numbers before citing.*

A representative starting list for the geology and mineral resources of Nigeria. *Verify editions, years, and page numbers before citing.*

## General Nigerian geology & mineral resources
- **Obaje, N.G. (2009).** *Geology and Mineral Resources of Nigeria.* Lecture Notes in Earth Sciences 120, Springer. — The standard single-volume overview.
- **Wright, J.B.** — Classic work on Nigerian geology, lineaments, and fracture systems.
- **McCurry, P.** — Geology of the NW Nigerian basement (Zaria sector).
- **Rahaman, M.A.** — Reviews of the Basement Complex of Nigeria.
- **Geological Survey of Nigeria Agency (GSN/NGGS) Bulletins** — the primary agency series on rocks, minerals, and regional geology.
- **NGGS geological maps** — 1:2,000,000 national map and 1:100,000 map sheets.

## Basement Complex & the Pan-African Orogeny
- **Turner, D.C. (1983).** "Upper Proterozoic schist belts in the Nigerian sector of the Pan-African province of West Africa." *Precambrian Research*.
- **Matheis, G. & Turner, D.C.** — Lithostratigraphic and structural framework of the Nigerian schist belts.

## Younger Granite ring complexes
- **Bowden, P., van Breemen, O., Hutchinson, J. & Turner, D.C. (1976).** "Palaeozoic and Mesozoic age trends for some ring complexes in Niger and Nigeria." *Nature*.
- **Jacobson, R.R.E., MacLeod, W.N. & Black, R.** — Work on the Nigerian Younger Granites and associated Sn–Nb–Ta mineralization.

## Benue Trough
- **Petters, S.W. (1978).** "Stratigraphic evolution of the Benue Trough and its implications for the Upper Cretaceous palaeogeography of West Africa." *Journal of Geology*.
- **Obaje, N.G. et al.** — Biostratigraphy, geochemistry, and economic geology of the Benue Trough (coal, lead–zinc, salt, ironstone).

## Niger Delta
- **Short, K.C. & Stäuble, A.J. (1967).** "Outline of geology of Niger Delta." *AAPG Bulletin* 51(5).
- **Doust, H. & Omatsola, E. (1990).** "Niger Delta." In *Divergent/Passive Margin Basins*, AAPG Memoir 48.
- **Reijers, T.J.A., Petters, S.W. & Nwajide, C.S. (1997).** *The Niger Delta Basin.* In *African Basins* (ed. Selley), Elsevier.

## Chad & Sokoto Basins
- **Adekeye, O.A.** and others — Cretaceous/Tertiary stratigraphy and the Chad/Sokoto basins.

## Mineral deposits
- **Olade, M.A.** — Genesis of gold mineralization in the Nigerian schist belts; gold geochemistry.
- **Obaje, N.G. (2009)** and GSN bulletins — deposit-by-deposit descriptions (tin, columbite, gold, iron, lead–zinc, barite, gemstones, industrial minerals).

## Official data
- **USGS Minerals Yearbook** — annual *Nigeria* chapter (production & trade).
- **USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries** — tin, tantalum/niobium, gold, iron ore, etc.

## How to use them
Start with **Obaje (2009)** for a synthesis, then the **NGGS maps/bulletins** and **company reports** for locality detail, and the **USGS** for current production data.

**Suggested reading order:** (1) Obaje (2009) for the big picture → (2) NGGS map + relevant GSN bulletin for your area → (3) the specific paper above for your province → (4) USGS for the latest numbers.

## Facts
- **Obaje (2009)** is the standard single-volume synthesis of Nigerian geology and minerals.
- **GSN/NGGS bulletins** are the authoritative source for regional and deposit geology.
- The **USGS Minerals Yearbook** (Nigeria chapter) tracks current production and trade.
- **Mindat.org** is the best free database for mineral localities.

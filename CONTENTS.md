# Geology & Mineral Resources of Nigeria — Comprehensive Field Handout

**A basics-to-expert guide for the exploration geologist in Nigeria.**
Emphasis on **identification** — every rock and mineral is illustrated (pure
specimen *and* as it occurs in rock), with profiles, host states, and field notes.

---

## How to read this handout

Every **rock** and **mineral** profile follows the same template so you can compare
specimens quickly in the field:

**Rock profile** → Composition · Texture · Diagnostic field features · Host
states/localities · Associated minerals · Economic relevance · Identification
notes · Images (hand specimen + in situ).

**Mineral profile** → Chemistry & mineralogy · Crystal habit · Physical properties
(and how to test them) · Occurrence with images (pure specimen + in rock/ore) ·
Genesis · States/localities · Mining, uses & market · Exploration notes.

> **Note on localities:** the states and classic localities given are
> representative of known occurrences. Always cross-check against the current
> **NGGS** (Nigerian Geological Survey Agency) geological map, GSN bulletins, and
> company reports before relying on them in the field. Images carry a source
> credit for attribution.

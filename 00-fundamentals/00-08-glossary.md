# 0.8 — Working Glossary

Key vocabulary for the handout. Terms appear across all parts; add to this list as
you go. **A–E · F–L · M–R · S–Z.**

## A–E

- **Alteration** — chemical change of a rock by fluids; a clue to nearby ore.
- **Anticline / Syncline** — up-arched / down-bent fold.
- **Aphanitic** — igneous texture with crystals too small to see (fast cooling).
- **Aquifer** — a rock/sediment that stores and transmits groundwater.
- **Assay** — laboratory measurement of a sample's metal content.
- **Basement Complex** — Nigeria's ancient crystalline Precambrian rocks.
- **Beneficiation** — physically upgrading ore (crushing, gravity, flotation) before smelting.
- **Borehole / drilling** — a narrow hole sunk to sample rock at depth.
- **Breccia** — rock of angular broken fragments (fault or volcanic).
- **Clastic** — sedimentary rock made of broken fragments of other rock.
- **Concentrate** — the metal-rich product of beneficiation.
- **Cut-off grade** — lowest grade that is economic to mine.
- **Dilution** — waste rock mined with ore, lowering feed grade.
- **Eon / Era / Period / Epoch** — divisions of geologic time, largest to smallest.
- **Erosion** — removal and transport of rock/soil by water, wind, ice, gravity.

## F–L

- **Fault** — fracture with movement (normal, reverse, strike-slip).
- **Foliation** — planar mineral alignment from pressure.
- **Gangue** — worthless minerals mixed with ore.
- **Geochemistry** — measuring element abundances to find anomalies.
- **Geophysics** — measuring physical properties (gravity, magnetics, resistivity, seismic).
- **Grade** — concentration of a valuable element.
- **Gossan** — rusty iron-rich cap over a weathered sulfide deposit.
- **Granite** — coarse-grained felsic igneous rock (quartz + feldspar + mica).
- **Greisen** — quartz–mica alteration rock around tin–tungsten granites.
- **Hydrothermal** — involving hot, mineral-bearing fluids.
- **Indicator minerals** — minerals that point to a deposit (e.g. pyrope garnet → kimberlite/diamond).
- **Intrusive / Extrusive** — igneous rock cooled below / at the surface.
- **Joint** — a fracture with no movement; lets fluids in.
- **Kimberlite** — ultramafic volcanic rock, the main host of diamond.
- **Laterite** — iron/aluminium-rich weathered layer in the tropics.
- **Lineament** — linear feature (often a fracture) on maps/imagery; controls fluids.
- **Lithification** — sediment turning to rock (compaction + cementation).
- **Lode** — a hard-rock, in-situ mineral deposit (vs placer).

## M–R

- **Magma / Lava** — molten rock below / at the surface.
- **Metamorphism** — rock change by heat/pressure/fluids without melting.
- **Mohs scale** — 1–10 mineral hardness scale.
- **Ore** — rock enriched enough to mine profitably.
- **Panning** — using water and a pan to concentrate heavy minerals.
- **Pegmatite** — very coarse-grained igneous rock, often ore-bearing (tin, gems).
- **Phaneritic** — igneous texture with visible crystals (slow cooling).
- **Placer** — deposit of dense grains concentrated by water/wind.
- **Plate tectonics** — the theory of moving lithospheric plates driving geology.
- **Protolith** — the original rock a metamorphic rock formed from.
- **Regolith** — the layer of weathered rock and soil above fresh rock.
- **Reserve / Resource** — economic part of a deposit / total estimated metal present.
- **Recovery** — fraction of metal actually extracted in processing.

## S–Z

- **Saprolite** — deeply weathered, in-place rotten rock in the regolith.
- **Schist** — medium-grade foliated metamorphic rock with aligned micas.
- **Sedimentary** — rock formed from deposited, lithified sediment.
- **Shear zone** — a zone of intense deformation; a major fluid pathway.
- **Specific gravity** — density relative to water; "heft" in the field.
- **Stockwork** — a mesh of many small veins through a rock volume.
- **Streak** — colour of a mineral's powder; tested on a streak plate.
- **Strike & Dip** — orientation of a tilted surface.
- **Superposition** — in undisturbed layers, oldest at the bottom.
- **Tailings** — the waste left after ore is processed.
- **Tenor** — the grade of the ore mineral itself.
- **Tonnage** — total quantity of ore (or contained metal).
- **Uniformitarianism** — present processes explain the past.
- **Vein** — a mineral-filled fracture.
- **Volcanogenic massive sulfide (VMS)** — sulfide ore formed on the seafloor from hot fluids.
- **Weathering** — breakdown of rock at the Earth's surface.
- **Younger Granites** — Jurassic ring-complex granites of the Jos Plateau, Nigeria.

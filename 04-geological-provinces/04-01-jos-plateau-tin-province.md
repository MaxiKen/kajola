# 4.1 — Jos Plateau / Younger Granite Tin–Columbite Province

## Overview
Nigeria's most famous mineral province — a cluster of **Jurassic Younger Granite ring
complexes** intruding the Basement Complex, centered on the high **Jos Plateau**. Once
one of the world's great **tin** producers.

## Geology
- **Younger Granites** (~200–140 Ma): ring-complex granites, syenites, and their
  **pegmatites and greisens** (see [The Younger Granites](../01-general-geology/01-03-younger-granite-ring-complexes.md)).
- Deep tropical weathering has produced **placers** in rivers and soils.

## Key minerals & resources
- **Cassiterite (tin)** — the historic backbone (see [Cassiterite](../03-mineral-resources/metallic/cassiterite.md)).
- **Columbite–tantalite (coltan)**, **wolframite (tungsten)**, **monazite**.
- **Gemstones** — **emerald, aquamarine, tourmaline, topaz, garnet** (see [Emerald](../03-mineral-resources/gemstones/emerald.md)).
- **Lead–zinc** (Zurak), **barite**, **granite** (dimension stone), **kaolin**, **clay**.
- **Lithium** potential in pegmatites (emerging).

## States
**Plateau** (core — Jos, Bukuru, Naraguta, Ropp, Wase), **Bauchi, Kaduna, Nasarawa**.

## Exploration notes
- Map and sample **ring complexes and zoned pegmatites**; **pan** rivers for cassiterite/
  columbite.
- Heavy-mineral and radiometric methods help; **black tourmaline (schorl)** and greisen
  alteration are useful indicators.

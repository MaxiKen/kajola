# 4.1 — Jos Plateau / Younger Granite Tin–Columbite Province

> **Type:** Granite ring-complex (anorogenic) province · **Age:** Jurassic (~200–140 Ma)
> **Quick summary:** Nigeria's most famous mineral province — a cluster of **Younger Granite ring complexes** intruding the Basement; once one of the world's great **tin** producers.

## At a glance

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Province type | Anorogenic **Younger Granite** ring complexes |
| Age | Jurassic (~200–140 Ma) |
| Core area | **Jos Plateau** (Plateau State) |
| Host / related rocks | Basement Complex; granite, syenite, **pegmatite, greisen** |
| Key resources | **Tin, coltan, tungsten**, gemstones, Pb–Zn, barite, kaolin |
| Economic role | Historic world-class tin producer; today artisanal + gem/rare-metal focus |

## Overview
Nigeria's most famous mineral province — a cluster of **Jurassic Younger Granite ring complexes** intruding the Basement Complex, centered on the high **Jos Plateau**. Once one of the world's great **tin** producers.

## Location & extent
Centered on the **Jos Plateau**, the province extends across **Plateau** (core — Jos, Bukuru, Naraguta, Ropp, Wase), **Bauchi, Kaduna, and Nasarawa**. The ring complexes sit within the wider Basement Complex.

## Geology
- **Younger Granites** (~200–140 Ma): ring-complex granites, syenites, and their **pegmatites and greisens** (see [The Younger Granites](../01-general-geology/01-03-younger-granite-ring-complexes.md)).
- The granites intrude older **Basement** rocks and are fractionated into concentric ring structures.
- Deep tropical weathering has produced **placers** in rivers and soils.

## Structure & tectonic setting
The ring complexes show classic **cone-sheet and ring-dyke** fracturing, with progressively evolved (rare-element) granite/pegmatite cores. This zoning concentrates **tin, niobium–tantalum, tungsten**, and **lithium** in the late, element-rich zones.

## Key minerals & resources
- **Cassiterite (tin)** — the historic backbone (see [Cassiterite](../03-mineral-resources/metallic/cassiterite.md)).
- **Columbite–tantalite (coltan)**, **wolframite (tungsten)**, **monazite** (see [Columbite–Tantalite](../03-mineral-resources/metallic/columbite-tantalite.md), [Wolframite & Scheelite](../03-mineral-resources/metallic/wolframite-scheelite.md)).
- **Gemstones** — **emerald, aquamarine, tourmaline, topaz, garnet** (see [Emerald](../03-mineral-resources/gemstones/emerald.md)).
- **Lead–zinc** (Zurak), **barite**, **granite** (dimension stone), **kaolin**, **clay**.
- **Lithium** potential in pegmatites (emerging).

## Notable localities
- **Jos / Bukuru / Naraguta / Ropp** — historic tin fields.
- **Zurak** — lead–zinc.
- Rare-element pegmatites — source of gems, coltan, and emerging lithium.

## Geological significance
A textbook example of **anorogenic (A-type) ring-complex magmatism** that fractionates rare elements (Sn, Nb–Ta, W, Li, Be, REE) into economic concentrations. It made Nigeria a top tin producer and remains Africa's premier rare-element pegmatite province.

## Exploration notes
- Map and sample **ring complexes and zoned pegmatites**; **pan** rivers for cassiterite/columbite.
- Heavy-mineral and radiometric methods help; **black tourmaline (schorl)** and greisen alteration are useful indicators.

## Field tips
- Follow **black tourmaline (schorl)** and **greisen** (quartz–mica) alteration to fertile pegmatite cores.
- Pan streams draining the ring complexes for heavy cassiterite/coltan grains.
- Look for gem beryl/tourmaline in the zoned pegmatite interiors.

## Facts
- Jos Plateau was once one of the **world's great tin producers**.
- The **Younger Granites** are ~200–140 Ma anorogenic ring complexes.
- Its **rare-element pegmatites** yield tin, coltan, tungsten, lithium, and gems.
- **Schorl** (black tourmaline) is a classic indicator of fertile pegmatites.

# 4.7 — Coastal & River Placer Belts

## Overview
Where weathering and water **concentrate dense minerals** into workable deposits.
Two settings: **coastal beach sands** and **river/terrace placers**.

## Coastal heavy-mineral (beach) sands
Concentrate **ilmenite, rutile, zircon, monazite, and magnetite** (the "black sands").

- **States:** **Lagos, Ondo, Delta, Rivers, Akwa Ibom, Cross River.**
- Resources: **titanium** (ilmenite/rutile), **zircon**, and **rare-earth** (monazite)
  (see [Titanium minerals](../03-mineral-resources/metallic/titanium-minerals.md),
  [Zircon & Monazite](../03-mineral-resources/metallic/zircon-monazite.md)).

## River / alluvial placers
- **Tin & columbite** in the **Jos Plateau** rivers — **Plateau, Bauchi** (see [Cassiterite](../03-mineral-resources/metallic/cassiterite.md)).
- **Gold** in rivers draining the schist belts — **Zamfara, Osun, Oyo, Kwara, Niger,
  Kebbi, Edo** (see [Gold](../03-mineral-resources/metallic/gold.md)).

## Exploration notes
- **Pan streams** and sample **black sands**; follow heavy minerals upstream.
- Placers form where water **slows** (river bends, beaches, terrace gravels)
  (see [Placers & alluvium](../02-rocks-of-nigeria/sedimentary/placers-alluvium.md)).

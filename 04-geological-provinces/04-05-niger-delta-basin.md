# 4.5 — The Niger Delta Basin (Petroleum & Gas)

## Overview
Africa's largest oil province and the powerhouse of Nigeria's economy — a
**Tertiary–Recent delta** built by the Niger/Benue river system.

## Geology
- **Benin Formation** — continental sands (youngest).
- **Agbada Formation** — alternating sand & shale (the main **reservoir**).
- **Akata Formation** — marine shale (the **source & seal**).
- Oil and gas are trapped in **growth-fault / rollover structures**
  (see [The Niger Delta Basin](../01-general-geology/01-04-sedimentary-basins.md)).

## Key resources
- **Crude oil** (see [Petroleum](../03-mineral-resources/energy/petroleum.md)).
- **Natural gas** — associated and non-associated; huge reserves
  (see [Natural gas](../03-mineral-resources/energy/natural-gas.md)).
- Minor **bitumen**, plus sand, clay, and kaolin.

## States
**Rivers, Bayelsa, Delta, Akwa Ibom, Cross River, Imo, Ondo** (onshore & offshore).

## Exploration notes
- Exploration is **seismic + drilling** focused on structural traps — a specialist
  petroleum play, not a surface-mineral one.
- **Offshore** (deep-water) is a major current frontier.

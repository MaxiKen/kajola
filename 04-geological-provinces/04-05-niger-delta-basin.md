# 4.5 — The Niger Delta Basin (Petroleum & Gas)

> **Type:** Tertiary–Recent delta basin · **Age:** Tertiary–Recent
> **Quick summary:** Africa's largest oil province and the powerhouse of Nigeria's economy — a **Tertiary–Recent delta** built by the Niger/Benue river system.

## At a glance

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Basin type | **Delta** (regressive Tertiary–Recent) |
| Age | Tertiary–Recent |
| Location | Southern Nigeria (onshore & offshore) |
| Host formations | Benin (continental), Agbada (reservoir), Akata (source/seal) |

## Overview
Africa's largest oil province and the powerhouse of Nigeria's economy — a **Tertiary–Recent delta** built by the Niger/Benue river system.

## Location & extent
**Rivers, Bayelsa, Delta, Akwa Ibom, Cross River, Imo, Ondo** — spanning onshore delta swamps and the offshore continental shelf and deep water.

## Geology
- **Benin Formation** — continental sands (youngest).
- **Agbada Formation** — alternating sand & shale (the main **reservoir**).
- **Akata Formation** — marine shale (the **source & seal**).
- Oil and gas are trapped in **growth-fault / rollover structures** (see [The sedimentary basins](../01-general-geology/01-04-sedimentary-basins.md)).

## Structure & tectonic setting
A **regressive delta** with classic **growth faults** and **rollover anticlines**. As the delta prograded, syndepositional faults created structural traps; interbedded shales provide seals, and the cyclic Agbada sands provide reservoirs.

## Key resources
- **Crude oil** (see [Petroleum](../03-mineral-resources/energy/petroleum.md)).
- **Natural gas** — associated and non-associated; huge reserves (see [Natural gas](../03-mineral-resources/energy/natural-gas.md)).
- Minor **bitumen**, plus sand, clay, and kaolin.

## Notable localities
- Onshore fields across **Rivers, Bayelsa, Delta**.
- **Offshore deep-water** — a major current frontier.
- Gas provinces (associated and non-associated) across the delta.

## Geological significance
The Niger Delta is Africa's largest petroleum province and the backbone of Nigeria's economy. Its **Akata–Agbada–Benin** source–reservoir–seal system is a world-class petroleum system, and it holds some of Africa's largest gas reserves.

## Exploration notes
- Exploration is **seismic + drilling** focused on structural traps — a specialist petroleum play, not a surface-mineral one.
- **Offshore** (deep-water) is a major current frontier.
- Reducing **gas flaring** and monetizing gas is a national priority.

## Field tips
- Petroleum exploration is **geophysics-led** (seismic reflection) — map growth faults and rollover traps.
- Understand the **source (Akata) → reservoir (Agbada) → seal/trap → overburden (Benin)** system.
- Not a hand-specimen exercise — this is a subsurface, drilling-based play.

## Facts
- The Niger Delta is **Africa's largest oil province**.
- Its petroleum system: **Akata** (source) → **Agbada** (reservoir) → **Benin** (overburden).
- Oil/gas are trapped in **growth-fault / rollover structures**.
- It holds some of **Africa's largest gas reserves**; deep-water offshore is the frontier.

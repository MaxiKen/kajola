## Overview
The ancient **Precambrian crystalline foundation** underlies about half of Nigeria — the northern, central, eastern, and western regions. It is not uniform: it comprises several rock associations (terranes) that control what minerals you'll find.

## Location & extent
Underlies the **northern, central, eastern, and western** Basement regions — roughly the western half of Nigeria plus the north and east, interrupted by the sedimentary basins (Benue, Chad, Niger Delta, Sokoto/Dahomey).

## The main rock associations
- **Migmatite–gneiss core** — deeply metamorphosed, banded rocks (see [Migmatite](../02-rocks-of-nigeria/metamorphic/migmatite.md), [Banded gneiss](../02-rocks-of-nigeria/metamorphic/banded-gneiss.md)).
- **Schist belts** — narrow metavolcanic–metasedimentary belts (gold!) (see [Mica schist & Phyllite](../02-rocks-of-nigeria/metamorphic/mica-schist-phyllite.md)).
- **Older Granites** — intrusive granitoids (see [Granite](../02-rocks-of-nigeria/igneous/granite.md)).
- **Charnockite massifs** — high-grade granulites, mostly in the north (see [Charnockite](../02-rocks-of-nigeria/igneous/charnockite.md)).
- **Pegmatite fields** — rare-element veins around granites (see [Pegmatite](../02-rocks-of-nigeria/igneous/pegmatite.md)).
- **Quartzite/quartz schist, amphibolite, marble** — metamorphosed sediments/mafics.

## What to expect where (general)
- **North / north-central** (Kaduna, Plateau, Bauchi, Katsina, Kano): granites, charnockite, migmatite; **Younger Granites** on the Jos Plateau → **tin, columbite**.
- **Southwest** (Osun, Oyo, Kwara, Ekiti, Ondo): **schist belts** → **gold**; granite, marble, quartzite.
- **East / southeast** (Enugu, Ebonyi, Cross River, Edo): Basement mixed with Benue sediments → **lead-zinc, gold, limestone**.
- Everywhere: deep **laterite/regolith**, **granite** for dimension stone, and **kaolin/clay** from feldspar weathering.

## Why it matters (terrane → target)
Knowing the **terrane** tells you the likely mineral **target**:
- **Schist belt** → **gold** (quartz veins along shears).
- **Granite / pegmatite** → **tin, columbite, tungsten, gems, lithium**.
- **Charnockite / high-grade core** → migmatite-hosted mineralization.
- **Contact (marble–granite) zone** → **skarn** minerals.
- **Deep regolith** → **laterite iron, kaolin, bauxite, placer** minerals.

## Exploration approach
- Map the **terrane** first, then target its characteristic minerals.
- In **schist belts**, follow quartz veins/shears and pan streams (gold).
- Around **granites/pegmatites**, sample zoned pegmatites and pan for cassiterite/coltan.
- Use **geochemistry** and **heavy-mineral/panning** surveys across the terrane.

## Field tips
- Identify the host rock first (schist vs granite vs gneiss) — it points to the target.
- **Pan streams** draining the terrane; heavy minerals reveal what's upstream.
- Look for **laterite/regolith** as a near-surface concentrator.

## Facts
- The Basement underlies **about half of Nigeria**.
- It is a mosaic of **terranes** — schist belts, migmatite–gneiss, granites, charnockite, pegmatites.
- The **terrane controls the mineral target** (schist → gold; pegmatite → tin/gems).
- Deep **tropical weathering** adds laterite iron, kaolin, and placers.

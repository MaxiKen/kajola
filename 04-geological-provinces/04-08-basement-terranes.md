# 4.8 — The Basement Complex Terranes (a state-by-state rock picture)

## Overview
The ancient **Precambrian crystalline foundation** underlies about half of Nigeria —
the northern, central, eastern, and western regions. It is not uniform: it comprises
several rock associations (terranes) that control what minerals you'll find.

## The main rock associations
- **Migmatite–gneiss core** — deeply metamorphosed, banded rocks
  (see [Migmatite](../02-rocks-of-nigeria/metamorphic/migmatite.md), [Gneiss](../02-rocks-of-nigeria/metamorphic/banded-gneiss.md)).
- **Schist belts** — narrow metavolcanic–metasedimentary belts (gold!)
  (see [Mica schist](../02-rocks-of-nigeria/metamorphic/mica-schist-phyllite.md)).
- **Older Granites** — intrusive granitoids (see [Granite](../02-rocks-of-nigeria/igneous/granite.md)).
- **Charnockite massifs** — high-grade granulites, mostly in the north
  (see [Charnockite](../02-rocks-of-nigeria/igneous/charnockite.md)).
- **Pegmatite fields** — rare-element veins around granites
  (see [Pegmatite](../02-rocks-of-nigeria/igneous/pegmatite.md)).
- **Quartzite/quartz schist, amphibolite, marble** — metamorphosed sediments/mafics.

## What to expect where (general)
- **North / north-central** (Kaduna, Plateau, Bauchi, Katsina, Kano): granites,
  charnockite, migmatite; **Younger Granites** on the Jos Plateau → **tin, columbite**.
- **Southwest** (Osun, Oyo, Kwara, Ekiti, Ondo): **schist belts** → **gold**; granite,
  marble, quartzite.
- **East / southeast** (Enugu, Ebonyi, Cross River, Edo): Basement mixed with Benue
  sediments → **lead-zinc, gold, limestone**.
- Everywhere: deep **laterite/regolith**, **granite** for dimension stone, and
  **kaolin/clay** from feldspar weathering.

## Why it matters
Knowing the **terrane** tells you the likely mineral **target** — schist belt → gold;
granite/pegmatite → tin/columbite/gems; contact zone → skarn minerals.

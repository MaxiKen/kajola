## Additional resources (profiled in Part III)
- **Bauxite** — Ekiti, Ondo, Adamawa, Plateau, Oyo
- **Copper / Chalcopyrite** — Plateau, Bauchi, Zamfara, Nasarawa
- **Silver** — Plateau, Zamfara, Niger *(byproduct)*
- **Fluorite** — Plateau, Taraba
- **Graphite** — Kaduna, Niger, Plateau, Kogi
- **Mica** — Kogi, Nasarawa, Kaduna, Bauchi, Niger, Plateau, Kwara
- **Sillimanite** — Plateau, Kogi, Oyo
- **Pyrite / Marcasite / Pyrrhotite** — widespread with sulfides (esp. Ebonyi, Benue, Plateau, Nasarawa)
- **Bismuth** — Plateau, Nasarawa *(byproduct)*
- **Calcite** — with limestone (Ogun, Kogi, Cross River, Edo)
- **Dolomite** — Kogi, Oyo, Niger, FCT
- **Fire clay** — Kogi, Enugu, Benue
- **Marl** — Sokoto, Borno
- **Trona** — Borno, Yobe
- **Brine** — Ebonyi, Benue, Nasarawa, Cross River (+ Lake Chad/Borno)
- **Dimension stone** — Ogun, Oyo, Kogi, Ebonyi, Ondo, FCT
- **Diamond** — *none proven (trace in Plateau, Bauchi — watch mineral only)*

## Quick "headline" summary by region

- **Oil & gas:** Rivers, Bayelsa, Delta, Akwa Ibom, Imo, Cross River, Ondo.
- **Tin / columbite / gems:** Plateau (core), Bauchi, Kaduna, Nasarawa, Kano.
- **Gold:** Zamfara, Kebbi, Osun, Oyo, Niger, Kwara, Kogi, Katsina, Edo, Ebonyi.
- **Coal:** Enugu, Kogi, Nasarawa, Anambra, Benue, Gombe.
- **Lead–zinc:** Ebonyi, Enugu, Plateau, Benue, Cross River, Taraba.
- **Iron ore:** Kogi (Itakpe), Enugu, Nasarawa, plus lateritic iron statewide.
- **Limestone (cement):** Ogun, Enugu, Benue, Kogi, Cross River, Sokoto, Borno.
- **Gypsum:** Gombe, Nasarawa, Bauchi, Borno, Sokoto, Yobe, Taraba.
- **Bitumen:** Ondo (major), Edo, Lagos, Ogun.
- **Phosphate:** Sokoto, Ogun.
- **Kaolin / clay:** Ondo, Ekiti, Ogun, Edo, Delta, Oyo, Kwara, Kogi (and many more).

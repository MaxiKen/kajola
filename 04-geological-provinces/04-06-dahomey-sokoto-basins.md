# 4.6 — The Dahomey & Sokoto (Iullemmeden) Basins

> **Two basins:** Dahomey (SW — bitumen) & Sokoto/Iullemmeden (NW — phosphate)
> **Quick summary:** Two flanking basins — the **Dahomey** (SW) holds Nigeria's big **bitumen** and cement/kaolin; the **Sokoto (Iullemmeden)** (NW) is Nigeria's main **phosphate** (fertilizer) province.

## At a glance

| Attribute | Dahomey Basin | Sokoto (Iullemmeden) Basin |
|---|---|---|
| Location | Southwest (Lagos, Ogun, Ondo, Edo) | Northwest (Sokoto, Zamfara, Kebbi, Katsina) |
| Age | Cretaceous–Tertiary | Phanerozoic |
| Headline resource | **Bitumen / tar sands** (Ondo) | **Phosphate** (Dange/Awwal) |
| Other resources | Lignite, clay, kaolin, limestone, phosphate | Gypsum, limestone, clay, kaolin, ironstone, lignite, salt |
| Economic role | Future unconventional oil; cement & kaolin | Nigeria's main fertilizer (phosphate) province |

## Overview
Two sedimentary basins on Nigeria's flanks: the **Dahomey Basin** (southwest, extending in from Benin Republic/Togo) and the **Sokoto (Iullemmeden) Basin** (northwest). Together they anchor Nigeria's bitumen and phosphate potential.

## The Dahomey Basin (Southwest)
A Cretaceous–Tertiary basin extending in from Benin Republic/Togo.
- **Bitumen / tar sands** — very large deposits in **Ondo State** (see [Bitumen](../03-mineral-resources/energy/bitumen.md)).
- **Lignite, clay, kaolin, phosphate (Ijebu-Ode, Ogun), limestone.**
- **States:** **Lagos, Ogun, Ondo, Edo.**

## The Sokoto (Iullemmeden) Basin (Northwest)
A Phanerozoic basin in NW Nigeria.
- **Phosphate** — Dange/Awwal (Sokoto) — a major national resource (see [Phosphate](../03-mineral-resources/industrial/phosphate.md)).
- **Gypsum, limestone, clay, kaolin, ironstone, lignite, salt.**
- **States:** **Sokoto, Zamfara, Kebbi, Katsina.**

## Geology & structure
- **Dahomey:** Cretaceous–Tertiary coastal/shelf sediments; the updip, biodegraded oil became **bitumen** (tar sands).
- **Sokoto (Iullemmeden):** Phanerozoic continental–marine sediments with marine **phosphate** beds; a foreland-style basin.

## Key minerals & resources
- **Bitumen** — Ondo (Dahomey); a future unconventional-oil resource (see [Bitumen](../03-mineral-resources/energy/bitumen.md)).
- **Phosphate** — Dange/Awwal, Sokoto; Ijebu-Ode, Ogun (see [Phosphate](../03-mineral-resources/industrial/phosphate.md)).
- **Limestone/cement, kaolin, clay, gypsum, lignite, ironstone, salt.**

## Notable localities
- **Ondo (Akoko/Ikale)** — Nigeria's biggest bitumen/tar-sand belt.
- **Dange / Awwal (Sokoto)** — main phosphate district.
- **Ijebu-Ode (Ogun)** — phosphate.

## Why they matter
- The **Dahomey** basin holds Nigeria's big **bitumen** (future unconventional oil) and important **limestone/cement** and **kaolin**.
- The **Sokoto** basin is Nigeria's main **phosphate** (fertilizer) province — vital to food security.

## Exploration notes
- **Bitumen:** black, sticky, petroleum-smelling sand; development depends on recovery technology and oil price.
- **Phosphate:** assess **P₂O₅ grade** and bed thickness in marine sedimentary sequences.
- **Limestone/kaolin:** apply the acid-fizz test and whiteness/iron assays.

## Field tips
- In the **Dahomey** basin, look for black, sticky, petroleum-smelling **tar sands**.
- In the **Sokoto** basin, target **nodular phosphate beds** and assess P₂O₅.
- Use the **acid-fizz test** to confirm limestone vs non-fizzing beds.

## Facts
- The **Dahomey** basin (SW) holds Nigeria's biggest **bitumen** deposits (Ondo).
- The **Sokoto (Iullemmeden)** basin (NW) is Nigeria's main **phosphate** province.
- Phosphate is essential for **fertilizer** and food security.
- Both basins also yield **limestone, kaolin, clay, and gypsum**.

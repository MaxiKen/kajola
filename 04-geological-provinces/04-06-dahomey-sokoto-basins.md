# 4.6 — The Dahomey & Sokoto (Iullemmeden) Basins

## The Dahomey Basin (Southwest)
A Cretaceous–Tertiary basin extending in from Benin Republic/Togo.

- **Bitumen / tar sands** — very large deposits in **Ondo State** (see [Bitumen](../03-mineral-resources/energy/bitumen.md)).
- **Lignite, clay, kaolin, phosphate (Ijebu-Ode, Ogun), limestone.**
- **States:** **Lagos, Ogun, Ondo, Edo.**

## The Sokoto (Iullemmeden) Basin (Northwest)
A Phanerozoic basin in NW Nigeria.

- **Phosphate** — Dange/Awwal (Sokoto) — a major national resource
  (see [Phosphate](../03-mineral-resources/industrial/phosphate.md)).
- **Gypsum, limestone, clay, kaolin, ironstone, lignite, salt.**
- **States:** **Sokoto, Zamfara, Kebbi, Katsina.**

## Why they matter
- The **Dahomey** basin holds Nigeria's big **bitumen** (future unconventional oil) and
  important **limestone/cement** and **kaolin**.
- The **Sokoto** basin is Nigeria's main **phosphate** (fertilizer) province.

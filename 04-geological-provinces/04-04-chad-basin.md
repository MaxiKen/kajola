# 4.4 — The Chad (Borno) Basin

> **Type:** Cretaceous–Recent continental/lacustrine basin · **Age:** Cretaceous–Recent
> **Quick summary:** Nigeria's **northeastern** sedimentary basin around **Lake Chad** — a key area for **evaporites, uranium, and gas**.

## At a glance

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Basin type | Continental / lacustrine sedimentary basin |
| Age | Cretaceous–Recent (Chad Formation) |
| Location | Northeastern Nigeria (around Lake Chad) |
| Host rocks | Chad Formation (continental/lacustrine) + older Cretaceous units |
| Key resources | **Gypsum, salt, uranium**, limestone, lignite, diatomite, kaolin, gas |
| Economic role | Major national **gypsum** source; emerging gas & uranium frontier |

## Overview
Nigeria's **northeastern** sedimentary basin, filled with Cretaceous to Recent sediments (notably the **Chad Formation**) around **Lake Chad**. A key area for evaporites, uranium, and gas.

## Location & extent
**Borno, Yobe, Bauchi, Gombe, Jigawa** — centered on the Lake Chad lowlands, extending westward into the Sokoto Basin region.

## Geology
- **Cretaceous–Tertiary** continental and lacustrine sediments (the Chad Formation), with older Cretaceous units beneath.
- Bordered by the Basement Complex and the **Biu Plateau** volcanics (see [Cenozoic volcanism](../01-general-geology/01-05-cenozoic-volcanism.md)).
- The lake (and its evaporated predecessors) deposited thick evaporite and lacustrine sequences.

## Structure & tectonic setting
A broad, gentle **sag/foreland-style basin** filling around Lake Chad, bordered by the Basement and the Cenozoic **Biu Plateau** volcanic field. The interplay of lakes, evaporation, and volcanism created its evaporite and diatomite deposits.

## Key minerals & resources
- **Gypsum** — Nafada, Dukku (Gombe) — a major national source (see [Gypsum](../03-mineral-resources/industrial/gypsum.md)).
- **Salt, limestone, uranium, lignite, diatomite, kaolin, clay** (see [Salt](../03-mineral-resources/industrial/salt.md), [Diatomite](../03-mineral-resources/industrial/diatomite.md), [Uranium](../03-mineral-resources/metallic/uranium.md)).
- **Natural gas** (non-associated) — an emerging frontier (see [Natural gas](../03-mineral-resources/energy/natural-gas.md)).

## Notable localities
- **Nafada, Dukku** (Gombe) — Nigeria's main gypsum district.
- **Lake Chad** — salt and brine.
- Deeper Cretaceous intervals — potential non-associated gas.

## Geological significance
The Chad Basin is Nigeria's leading **evaporite** province (gypsum, salt, brine) and hosts **lacustrine diatomite**. Its deep Cretaceous fill is an emerging **non-associated gas** and **uranium** frontier.

## Exploration notes
- **Evaporites** (gypsum, salt) are the headline — soft, non-fizzing beds.
- **Radiometrics** for uranium; assess gas potential in the deeper Cretaceous.
- Diatomite and clay occur in lacustrine settings.

## Field tips
- For **gypsum**, look for soft, non-fizzing white beds (vs fizzing limestone).
- Use **radiometrics** and a Geiger counter in pegmatite/sandstone units for uranium.
- Sample **black sands / lacustrine** layers for diatomite and heavy minerals.

## Facts
- The Chad Basin is Nigeria's main **gypsum** province (Nafada, Dukku).
- **Lake Chad** yields salt and brine by evaporation.
- It is an emerging **non-associated gas** and **uranium** frontier.
- **Biu Plateau** volcanics border the basin.

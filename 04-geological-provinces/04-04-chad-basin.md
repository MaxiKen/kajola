# 4.4 — The Chad (Borno) Basin

## Overview
Nigeria's **northeastern** sedimentary basin, filled with Cretaceous to Recent
sediments (notably the **Chad Formation**) around **Lake Chad**. A key area for
evaporites, uranium, and gas.

## Geology
- **Cretaceous–Tertiary** continental and lacustrine sediments (the Chad Formation),
  with older Cretaceous units beneath.
- Bordered by the Basement Complex and the **Biu Plateau** volcanics
  (see [Cenozoic volcanism](../01-general-geology/01-05-cenozoic-volcanism.md)).

## Key minerals & resources
- **Gypsum** — Nafada, Dukku (Gombe) — a major national source (see [Gypsum](../03-mineral-resources/industrial/gypsum.md)).
- **Salt, limestone, uranium, lignite, diatomite, kaolin, clay**.
- **Natural gas** (non-associated) — an emerging frontier.

## States
**Borno, Yobe, Bauchi, Gombe, Jigawa.**

## Exploration notes
- **Evaporites** (gypsum, salt) are the headline — soft, non-fizzing beds.
- **Radiometrics** for uranium; assess gas potential in the deeper Cretaceous.
- Diatomite and clay occur in lacustrine settings.

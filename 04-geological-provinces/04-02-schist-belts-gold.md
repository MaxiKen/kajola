# 4.2 — The Southwest Nigeria Schist Belts (Gold)

> **Type:** Metamorphic schist belts · **Age:** Precambrian (Basement)
> **Quick summary:** Nigeria's premier **hard-rock (lode) gold** ground — narrow NE–SW **schist belts** hosting quartz-vein gold, heavily worked by artisanal miners.

## At a glance

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Province type | Metamorphic **schist belts** (metavolcanic–metasedimentary) |

## Overview
Nigeria's premier **hard-rock (lode) gold** ground. Narrow, NE–SW **schist belts** — metavolcanic–metasedimentary sequences — cut through the Basement Complex and host quartz-vein gold, now heavily worked by artisanal miners.

## Location & extent
**Osun, Oyo, Kwara, Kogi, Niger, Edo, Zamfara, Katsina, Kebbi**, and adjoining areas. The belts form narrow, elongate strips across the southwest and northwest Basement.

## The main belts
- **Ilesa–Ife / Ilesha schist belt** — Osun, Oyo (Nigeria's most productive gold belt).
- **Egbe–Isanlu schist belt** — Kogi, Kwara.
- **Igarra schist belt** — Edo.
- **Maru / Anka schist belts** — Zamfara, Katsina, Kebbi (major artisanal gold rush area).

## Geology
- **Mica schist, phyllite, quartzite** and metasediments, cut by **quartz veins** and **shear zones** (see [Mica schist & Phyllite](../02-rocks-of-nigeria/metamorphic/mica-schist-phyllite.md)).
- Gold is **structurally controlled** — follow the veins and shears (see [Structural controls](../01-general-geology/01-06-structural-controls.md)).

## Structure & tectonic setting
The NE–SW belts are linear, strongly deformed zones of folded and sheared metasediments/metavolcanics. **Gold is structurally controlled** — concentrated in **quartz veins** along **shear zones** and faults, which act as fluid conduits.

## Key minerals & resources
- **Gold** — lode (quartz veins) and **placer** (rivers) (see [Gold](../03-mineral-resources/metallic/gold.md)).
- **Columbite**, **kyanite**, **garnet**, minor gemstones (see [Kyanite](../03-mineral-resources/gemstones/kyanite.md)).

## Notable localities
- **Ilesha / Ilesa** (Osun) — Nigeria's most productive gold belt.
- **Maru / Anka** (Zamfara) — major artisanal gold-rush area.
- **Egbe–Isanlu** (Kogi/Kwara), **Igarra** (Edo).

## Geological significance
These belts are Nigeria's most important **lode-gold** terranes, comparable to other West African Birimian greenstone/schist gold provinces. They host both hard-rock (quartz-vein) and secondary (placer) gold.

## Exploration notes
- **Pan streams** and follow gold upstream to its lode source.
- **Soil/rock geochemistry** for **Au + pathfinders (As, Sb)** along the belts.
- Rusty **gossans** and **quartz veins** near shears are prime targets (see [Gold](../03-mineral-resources/metallic/gold.md)).

## Field tips
- **Pan streams** and trace gold upstream to the lode source.
- Follow rusty **gossans** and **quartz veins** aligned with **shear zones**.
- Confirm gold by its softness, malleability, weight, and lack of tarnish (vs pyrite).

## Facts
- The schist belts are Nigeria's premier **lode (hard-rock) gold** ground.
- Gold is **structurally controlled** — follow veins and shear zones.
- **Maru/Anka (Zamfara)** is a major artisanal gold-rush area.
- Both **lode** (vein) and **placer** (river) gold occur.

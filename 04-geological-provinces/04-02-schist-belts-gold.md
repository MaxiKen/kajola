# 4.2 — The Southwest Nigeria Schist Belts (Gold)

## Overview
Nigeria's premier **hard-rock (lode) gold** ground. Narrow, NE–SW **schist belts** —
metavolcanic–metasedimentary sequences — cut through the Basement Complex and host
quartz-vein gold, now heavily worked by artisanal miners.

## The main belts
- **Ilesa–Ife / Ilesha schist belt** — Osun, Oyo (Nigeria's most productive gold belt).
- **Egbe–Isanlu schist belt** — Kogi, Kwara.
- **Igarra schist belt** — Edo.
- **Maru / Anka schist belts** — Zamfara, Katsina, Kebbi (major artisanal gold rush area).

## Geology
- **Mica schist, phyllite, quartzite** and metasediments, cut by **quartz veins** and
  **shear zones** (see [Mica schist](../02-rocks-of-nigeria/metamorphic/mica-schist-phyllite.md)).
- Gold is **structurally controlled** — follow the veins and shears
  (see [Structural controls](../01-general-geology/01-06-structural-controls.md)).

## Key minerals & resources
- **Gold** — lode (quartz veins) and **placer** (rivers).
- **Columbite**, **kyanite**, **garnet**, minor gemstones.

## States
**Osun, Oyo, Kwara, Kogi, Niger, Edo, Zamfara, Katsina, Kebbi**, and adjoining areas.

## Exploration notes
- **Pan streams** and follow gold upstream to its lode source.
- **Soil/rock geochemistry** for **Au + pathfinders (As, Sb)** along the belts.
- Rusty **gossans** and **quartz veins** near shears are prime targets
  (see [Gold](../03-mineral-resources/metallic/gold.md)).

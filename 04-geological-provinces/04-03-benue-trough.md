# 4.3 — The Benue Trough

## Overview
A vast **NE–SW Cretaceous rift/sag basin** (~800 km) that splits the Basement Complex
into a northwestern and southeastern half. It holds an exceptional **diversity** of
resources — coal, lead–zinc, salt, limestone, iron, barite, and more.

## Geology
- **Cretaceous sediments**: Bima Sandstone (continental), then marine/paralic
  **Yolde, Abakaliki, and Enugu** formations (sandstone, shale, limestone, coal).
- Folded and faulted along its margins (see [Sedimentary basins](../01-general-geology/01-04-sedimentary-basins.md)).

## Key minerals & resources
- **Coal** — Enugu, Kogi (Ogboyaga), Nasarawa (Lafia/Obi) (see [Coal](../03-mineral-resources/energy/coal.md)).
- **Lead–zinc** — Abakaliki, Ishiagu (Enugu), Zurak (Plateau) (see [Galena & Sphalerite](../03-mineral-resources/metallic/galena-sphalerite.md)).
- **Salt** — Awe (Nasarawa); **Limestone** — Nkalagu, Gboko, Igalamela.
- **Iron ore** — Itakpe, Agbaja, Ajabanoko (Kogi) (see [Iron ore](../03-mineral-resources/metallic/iron-ore.md)).
- **Barite, gypsum, clay, phosphate, uranium**, and bitumen shows.

## States
**Benue, Kogi, Enugu, Ebonyi, Nasarawa, Plateau (south), Taraba, Cross River, Anambra.**

## Exploration notes
- Sediment-hosted deposits — use **geology mapping, geochemistry, and drilling**.
- Follow **gossans and quartz/calcite veins** for Pb–Zn; assess **coal seams** and
  **limestone** beds; evaporites for salt/gypsum.

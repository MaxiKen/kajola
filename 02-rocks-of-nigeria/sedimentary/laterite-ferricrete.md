# Laterite & Ferricrete

> **Family:** Residual / sedimentary · **Origin:** intense tropical weathering

## Composition
Iron- and aluminium-rich material left behind as soluble elements are stripped from
rock by tropical weathering. **Laterite** is Al–Fe rich; **ferricrete** is an
iron-cemented **duricrust** (hard cap).

## Texture
**Pisolitic (pea-like) or nodular**, with a hard crust over a softer, paler
"saprolite/pallid zone." Reddish-brown; heavy; sometimes honeycombed.

## Diagnostic field features
- **Reddish-brown** color; **nodular/pisolitic** texture.
- A **hard iron cap** (ferricrete) over softer weathered rock.
- Heavy and iron-rich.

## Host states / localities
Blankets much of the **Basement Complex** under tropical weathering: **Plateau,
Kaduna, Oyo, Ondo, Ekiti, Kwara, Niger, Kogi, Nasarawa**, and most basement states.

## Associated minerals
Concentrated **iron, aluminium (bauxite potential), manganese, gold**, and residual
**kaolin**; **cassiterite** in some lateritized pegmatite areas.

## Economic relevance
- **Lateritic iron** and potential **bauxite (aluminium)**.
- **Road-base / construction material**.
- **Residual concentration** of gold, manganese, and other metals — important in
  exploration (see **§1.11**).

## Identification notes
- **Reddish + nodular/pisolitic + hard crust** = laterite/ferricrete.
- It is a **weathered** material, not a primary bedrock — sample the profile.
- Heavy iron enrichment at the top of the profile.

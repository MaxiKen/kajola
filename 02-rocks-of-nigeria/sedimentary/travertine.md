# Travertine (Calcareous Tufa)

## Composition
**Calcium carbonate (CaCO₃)** deposited from **spring, hot-spring, or cave** water —
chemically (and often biologically) precipitated calcite.

## Texture
Porous, **banded/laminated**, and cellular; frequently moulded around plants (tufa).

## Diagnostic field features
- Banded, porous calcite crust; **fizzes** in dilute acid.
- Associated with springs, waterfalls, and caves.

## Host states / localities
Localized to **springs and carbonate terrains** — parts of **Plateau** and the carbonate
areas of the Basement and sedimentary basins (see [Limestone & Dolomite](limestone.md)).

## Associated minerals
Limestone, calcite (see [Calcite](../../03-mineral-resources/industrial/calcite.md)).

## Economic relevance
**Ornamental/dimension stone** and lime; an indicator of active carbonate groundwater.

## Identification notes
- Banded, porous CaCO₃ that fizzes in acid; formed at springs/caves rather than in beds.

# Clay & Kaolin

> **Family:** Sedimentary / residual · **Composition:** clay minerals (kaolinite group)

## Composition
Fine-grained **hydrous aluminium silicates** — chiefly **kaolinite**, plus
illite, smectite (montmorillonite), and halloysite. **Kaolin** is the white,
high-kaolinite variety from feldspar weathering.

## Texture
**Fine, earthy, and plastic when wet**; dries hard; white (kaolin) to grey, red, or
brown (iron-stained clays).

## Diagnostic field features
- **Earthy** texture; **plastic and sticky when wet**, hard when dry.
- Gives off a characteristic **"clayey" smell** when breathed on/dampened.
- White kaolin **stains** and feels smooth/soapy.

## Host states / localities
Formed by intense tropical weathering of feldspar-rich rocks — widespread:
**Ondo, Ekiti, Ogun, Edo, Delta, Oyo, Osun, Kwara, Kogi, Enugu**, and more.

## Associated minerals
Quartz, feldspar (source), mica, iron oxides; associated with **laterite** profiles.

## Economic relevance
- **KAOLIN** — ceramics, paper coating, paint, rubber, plastics (a major industrial
  mineral).
- **Brick, pottery, and tile** clay.
- **Cement** raw material; **refractory** clays.

## Identification notes
- **Plastic when wet, earthy, fine** = clay.
- **White + soapy + plastic** = kaolin.
- **vs. siltstone:** clay is finer and more plastic; siltstone is slightly gritty.

# Greywacke

## Composition
A dark, **poorly sorted sandstone** rich in rock fragments and a clay matrix, with quartz
and feldspar — typical of **turbidite** (deep-water) sequences.

## Texture
Poorly sorted, gritty, and matrix-rich; darker and harder than clean sandstone.

## Diagnostic field features
- Dark, gritty, poorly sorted; feels abrasive.
- Darker and more "dirty" (matrix-rich) than a clean quartz sandstone.

## Host states / localities
Basement **metasedimentary** terrains (as **metagreywacke**) and some sedimentary
sequences — **Niger, Kwara, Kogi, Anambra, Benue**, etc.
(see [Metasediments](../metamorphic/metasediments.md)).

## Associated minerals
Shale and metasediments; with metamorphism becomes metagreywacke/quartz schist.

## Economic relevance
Aggregate and building stone; turbidite aquifers; can host mineralization (e.g., gold in
deformed equivalents).

## Identification notes
- Dark, poorly sorted, gritty sandstone with abundant matrix — the "dirty" end of the
  sandstone spectrum (see [Sandstone, Grit & Arkose](sandstone.md)).

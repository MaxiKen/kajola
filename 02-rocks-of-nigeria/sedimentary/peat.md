# Peat

## Composition
Partly decomposed **organic matter** (plant material) accumulated in waterlogged,
oxygen-poor settings. It is the **precursor to lignite and coal**.

## Texture
Fibrous, spongy, and soft; brown to black; very high moisture content.

## Diagnostic field features
- Brown/black, **fibrous** plant remains; light when dried; combustible.
- Forms in swamps, bogs, and waterlogged basins.

## Host states / localities
**Niger Delta and coastal wetlands** and some inland basins — **Delta, Bayelsa, Rivers,
Cross River, Akwa Ibom, Ondo**, etc.

## Associated minerals
Clay and alluvial/mangrove deposits; grades into [Coal & Lignite](coal.md) with burial.

## Economic relevance
Traditional **fuel**, horticultural growing medium, and a potential energy resource; the
first stage in coal formation.

## Identification notes
- Fibrous organic material in a wet setting; with burial and compaction it becomes
  lignite, then coal.

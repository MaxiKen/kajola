# Peridotite & Pyroxenite (Ultramafics)

> **Family:** Igneous — intrusive · **Composition:** Ultramafic (very low silica)

## Composition
- **Peridotite:** dominated by **olivine** + **pyroxene** (varieties: dunite ≈ all
  olivine; harzburgite = olivine + orthopyroxene; lherzolite = olivine + two pyroxenes).
- **Pyroxenite:** almost entirely **pyroxene** (± olivine).

## Texture
**Coarse-grained (phaneritic)**, often with crystals packed tightly; commonly
**serpentinized** (olivine → green, greasy serpentine).

## Diagnostic field features
- **Green (olivine)** + dark **pyroxene**; bright green where fresh.
- **Very dense and heavy**.
- Fresh olivine weathers to **serpentinite** — green, soft, greasy-feeling.
- Often forms **lenses/pods** within other rocks rather than large bodies.

## Host states / localities
Uncommon in Nigeria — occur as **ultramafic/ophiolite fragments** within some
**schist belts and suture zones** of the Basement (reported in parts of the
southwest and central basement). Minor, but economically interesting where found.

## Associated minerals
**Chromite** (in dunite/peridotite), **magnetite**, **nickel** (in serpentinized
peridotite), **asbestos (chrysotile)** and **talc** (from serpentine alteration).

## Economic relevance
- **Chromium (chromite), nickel, cobalt, magnesium**.
- **Talc, asbestos, serpentine** (ornamental stone).
- **PGE (platinum-group)** potential in some ultramafics.

## Identification notes
- **Green + heavy** = think olivine/peridotite.
- Serpentinized peridotite is **soft and greasy** (vs. hard fresh gabbro).
- Look for **chromite** as small black metallic grains.

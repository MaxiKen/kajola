# Basalt (the Younger Basalts)

> **Family:** Igneous — extrusive (volcanic) · **Composition:** Mafic

## Composition
**Plagioclase feldspar + pyroxene (augite)** ± **olivine** — the fine-grained
volcanic equivalent of **gabbro**. Silica-poor, iron/magnesium-rich.

## Texture
**Fine-grained (aphanitic)**, often **porphyritic**; may be **vesicular** (bubbly =
scoria) or show **columnar jointing** (hexagonal columns as it cooled).

## Diagnostic field features
- **Dark (black/dark grey), fine-grained, dense, and heavy**.
- Often **magnetic** (contains magnetite).
- May have **gas bubbles (vesicles)**, sometimes filled with white **zeolites** or
  calcite.
- Fresh surfaces are dark; weathers reddish-brown (iron oxidation).

## Host states / localities
Nigeria's **Younger Basalts**:
- **Jos Plateau** "Younger Basalts" capping the granite (**Plateau**, edges of Bauchi/Kaduna).
- **Biu Plateau** basalt field (**Borno, Yobe**).
- Also numerous **dykes** across the Basement.

## Associated minerals
**Olivine, augite, magnetite**; vesicles may hold **zeolites, calcite, agate**; rarely
native copper or other minor minerals.

## Economic relevance
- **Construction aggregate, road metal, railway ballast** — high value in Nigeria.
- **Dimension stone** (also sold as "black granite").
- Potential source of **copper/nickel** only rarely.

## Identification notes
- **Dark + fine-grained + dense/magnetic** = basalt.
- **vs. gabbro:** gabbro is coarse-grained (same minerals).
- **Vesicular/pumiceous** varieties float or are very light.

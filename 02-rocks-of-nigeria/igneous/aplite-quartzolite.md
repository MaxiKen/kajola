# Aplite & Quartzolite

## Composition
Fine-grained late-stage differentiates of granitic/pegmatitic systems:
- **Aplite** — a fine-grained ("sugary") **granite** (quartz + feldspar).
- **Quartzolite** — nearly pure **quartz**.

## Texture
**Aphanitic** (fine-grained) and equigranular — a "sugary" feel; quartzolite is massive,
white, vitreous quartz.

## Diagnostic field features
- Pale, fine, even-grained; occurs as **thin dikes/veins** at the margins of granites
  and around pegmatites.
- Sugary texture distinguishes it from coarse granite.

## Host states / localities
**Plateau, Bauchi, Kaduna, Nasarawa** — with the Younger Granites, Older Granites, and
their pegmatite fields.

## Associated minerals
Pegmatite, granite, quartz, feldspar, muscovite, tourmaline (see [Pegmatite](pegmatite.md)).

## Economic relevance
Minor in itself (a silica source), but a useful **indicator of granite/pegmatite
fertility** (tin, columbite, gems).

## Identification notes
- Fine sugary texture + pale colour + dike form; gradational with pegmatite and granite.

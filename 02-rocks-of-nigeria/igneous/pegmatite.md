# Pegmatite — the ore-bearing "giant crystal" rock

> **Family:** Igneous — intrusive (late-stage) · **Composition:** Granitic, enriched in rare elements

## Composition
Essentially **granitic** (quartz, feldspar, mica) but formed from the **water- and
element-rich last fluids** of a granite intrusion. Enriched in rare/incompatible
elements: **Li, Be, B, Sn, Nb, Ta, W, REE, U, Th**.

## Texture
**Extremely coarse-grained** — crystals from centimetres to several metres!
Often **zononed** (minerals change from wall to centre) and may show **graphic
texture** (quartz wedges intergrown with feldspar, like ancient writing).

## Diagnostic field features
- **Giant crystals** of feldspar, quartz, and mica (muscovite/lepidolite).
- Common **black tourmaline (schorl)** and **beryl**.
- Occurs as **veins, dykes, and pods** (not large masses).
- Often zoned — sample across the whole body.

## Host states / localities
A hallmark of the **Younger Granites** (Jos Plateau) and many Basement granites:
**Plateau, Bauchi, Kaduna, Nasarawa, Oyo, Kwara, Niger**, and gem-bearing pegmatites
in several states.

## Associated minerals
**Cassiterite (Sn), columbite–tantalite (Nb–Ta), wolframite (W), beryl, tourmaline,
spodumene/lepidolite (Li), monazite, zircon, topaz, gem minerals** (emerald,
aquamarine, garnet). Also industrial **feldspar, mica, quartz**.

## Economic relevance
**The single most important rock for Nigeria's rare-metal and gemstone wealth.**
Pegmatites are the primary source of **tin, columbite-tantalite, lithium, beryllium,
tungsten**, and many **gemstones** (see **Part III**).

## Identification notes
- **Huge crystals** + zoning = pegmatite.
- Hunt for **black tourmaline, micas, cassiterite, and beryl**.
- Sample the whole body — different minerals concentrate in different zones.

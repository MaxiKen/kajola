# Obsidian (Volcanic Glass)

## Composition
**Volcanic glass** — a rhyolitic (felsic) magma quenched so rapidly that it did not
crystallize; effectively natural, amorphous silica-rich glass.

## Texture
**Glassy**; breaks with a sharp **conchoidal fracture**; thin edges can be translucent.

## Diagnostic field features
- Black (sometimes brown/banded), glassy, conchoidal fracture, razor-sharp edges.
- Lacks crystals (unlike rhyolite).

## Host states / localities
Requires very rapid cooling of felsic magma; **rare in Nigeria** and not a significant
Nigerian rock — included for completeness
(see [Rhyolite, Trachyte & Phonolite](rhyolite-trachyte-phonolite.md)).

## Associated minerals
Rhyolite, pumice, perlite; spherulites may be present.

## Economic relevance
Ornamental stone, cutting tools (historically), and specialty/surgical glass.

## Identification notes
- Glassy lustre + conchoidal fracture + sharp edges; distinguish from quartz (crystalline)
  and from rhyolite (crystalline groundmass).

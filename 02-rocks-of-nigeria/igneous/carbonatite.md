# Carbonatite

## Composition
A rare **carbonate-rich igneous rock** — mostly **calcite, dolomite, or ankerite**, with
alkali minerals (biotite, amphibole, aegirine). Mantle-derived and associated with
alkaline complexes.

## Texture
Medium- to coarse-grained, massive, sometimes veined.

## Diagnostic field features
- A **carbonate rock of igneous origin** — it **fizzes** in acid but occurs as an igneous
  intrusion associated with alkaline rocks (the key clue).

## Host states / localities
Very rare and **not confirmed as significant in Nigeria**. It is a strategic "watch" rock
type found with alkaline complexes globally
(see [Syenite & Nepheline syenite](syenite-nepheline-syenite.md)).

## Associated minerals
Nepheline syenite, alkaline rocks; REE minerals (bastnäsite, monazite), niobium,
fluorite, apatite (see [Zircon & Monazite](../../03-mineral-resources/metallic/zircon-monazite.md),
[Fluorite](../../03-mineral-resources/industrial/fluorite.md)).

## Economic relevance
A major source of **rare-earth elements (REE)**, **niobium**, phosphate, fluorite, and
copper — strategically important.

## Identification notes
- Carbonate rock (fizzes) but with igneous texture/host and alkaline association —
  distinguishes it from sedimentary limestone/marble.

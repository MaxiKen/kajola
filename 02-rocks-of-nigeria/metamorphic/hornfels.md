# Hornfels

## Composition
A **contact-metamorphic** rock — the fine-grained, hard product of "baking" shale or other
argillaceous rock beside a hot intrusion. Minerals include **cordierite, andalusite,
biotite, and quartz**.

## Texture
**Massive, fine-grained, hard**, with a "horny" (splintery) fracture; **no foliation**.

## Diagnostic field features
- Very hard, dark, fine, massive.
- Occurs in the **contact aureole** around granites.
- "Spotted hornfels" shows small cordierite/andalusite porphyroblasts.

## Host states / localities
Around granite intrusions nationwide — the Basement and margins of Older/Younger Granites.

## Associated minerals
Granite, skarn, marble (in the contact zone) (see [Skarn](skarn.md), [Marble & Calc-silicate](marble-calc-silicate.md)).

## Economic relevance
An indicator of the **contact zone** (which may host skarn mineralization); occasionally
used as dimension stone.

## Identification notes
- Hard, massive, dark, fine; in a contact aureole; breaks with a splintery/conchoidal
  fracture rather than splitting along foliation.

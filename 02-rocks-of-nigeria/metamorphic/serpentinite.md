# Serpentinite

## Composition
A metamorphic/altered **ultramafic rock** composed mainly of **serpentine minerals**
(antigorite, chrysotile), formed by hydration/alteration of **peridotite/dunite**.

## Texture
Massive, sometimes **fibrous** (chrysotile asbestos); has a characteristic **greasy/waxy**
feel.

## Diagnostic field features
- **Green** (many shades — mottled green is typical).
- Softer and greasier than fresh peridotite; may show a serpentine "mesh" texture.

## Host states / localities
Basement areas containing ultramafics — parts of **Kaduna, Plateau**, and some schist
belts (see [Peridotite & Pyroxenite](../igneous/peridotite-pyroxenite.md)).

## Associated minerals
Peridotite, talc, chlorite, magnesite, chromite, nickel minerals
(see [Talc / Chlorite schist](talc-chlorite-schist.md)).

## Economic relevance
**Asbestos** (chrysotile), **nickel**, magnesite, and ornamental "serpentine" stone.

## Identification notes
- Green, greasy, softer than peridotite; forms by ultramafic alteration. Handle with care
  where fibrous (asbestos) varieties occur.

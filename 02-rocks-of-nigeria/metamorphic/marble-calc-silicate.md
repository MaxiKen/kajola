# Marble & Calc-Silicate Rock

> **Family:** Metamorphic · **Origin:** metamorphosed limestone/dolomite

## Composition
- **Marble:** recrystallized **calcite** and/or **dolomite**.
- **Calc-silicate rock:** marble rich in silicate minerals — **garnet (grossular),
  diopside, wollastonite, quartz, talc**.

## Texture
**Crystalline, interlocking** calcite grains — often coarse and sugary; commonly
**banded/veined** with colored impurities. Calc-silicate varieties have visible
garnet/pyroxene crystals.

## Diagnostic field features
- **Fizzes vigorously with dilute hydrochloric acid** (calcite/dolomite).
- **Soft** — easily scratched by a steel knife (hardness ~3).
- Can be carved/polished; may be white, grey, pink, or green.

## Host states / localities
Classic Nigerian marbles:
- **Igbeti** (Oyo)
- **Jakura** (Kogi)
- **Mega / Mure** (Kwara)
- plus limestone lenses in the Basement.

## Associated minerals
Garnet, diopside, wollastonite, quartz, talc, graphite.

## Economic relevance
- **Cement and lime** (calcium carbonate).
- **Ornamental/dimension stone** — tiles, sculpture, countertops.
- **Wollastonite and garnet** as industrial minerals.

## Identification notes
- **Acid fizz + soft (knife-scratchable) + crystalline** = marble.
- **Marble vs. quartzite:** quartzite doesn't fizz and is much harder.
- Green/pink marbles often owe their color to **grossular garnet or diopside**.

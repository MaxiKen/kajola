# Talc / Chlorite Schist (Soapstone)

> **Family:** Metamorphic · **Origin:** altered ultramafic/mafic rock (low-grade)

## Composition
Dominated by soft, platy minerals — **talc** and/or **chlorite** — with serpentine,
magnesite, and magnetite. Formed by hydration/alteration of ultramafic rocks
(peridotite/pyroxenite).

## Texture
**Schistose to massive**, soft, often greasy-feeling; green (chlorite) to pale
green/white (talc).

## Diagnostic field features
- **Very soft** — talc has hardness **1** (scratched by a fingernail).
- **Soapy/greasy feel** — hence "soapstone."
- **Green** color (chlorite/serpentine).

## Host states / localities
Altered ultramafic zones in the **Basement Complex**: reported in parts of **Kwara,
Oyo, Kaduna**, and some schist belts. Generally minor occurrences.

## Associated minerals
Talc, chlorite, serpentine, magnesite; sometimes **asbestos (chrysotile)**, **nickel,
chromite** (from the parent ultramafic).

## Economic relevance
- **Talc** — cosmetics, ceramics, paper, paint, plastics.
- **Soapstone** — carving, countertops, stove liners.
- **Asbestos** (handle with care) and **magnesia/magnesium** compounds.

## Identification notes
- **Soapy feel + very soft + green** = talc/chlorite schist (soapstone).
- Distinguished from chlorite-rich **greenstone** by extreme softness (talc).

# Mylonite & Cataclasite (Fault Rocks)

## Composition
Rocks formed by intense deformation in **fault/shear zones**:
- **Mylonite** — ductile deformation; fine-grained, foliated.
- **Cataclasite / fault breccia** — brittle deformation; angular clasts.

## Texture
Mylonite is fine-grained and foliated (often with **slickensided** surfaces and
surviving "porphyroclasts"); cataclasite is made of angular fragments in a fine matrix.

## Diagnostic field features
- Restricted to **faults and shear zones**.
- Sheared, fine texture; polished, striated **slickensides**; often flanked by quartz veins.

## Host states / localities
Nationwide along major shear zones — the **Basement** and especially the **schist belts**
(see [Structural controls](../../01-general-geology/01-06-structural-controls.md)).

## Associated minerals
Quartz veins, gold, sulfides (pyrite, chalcopyrite, galena) — fault zones channel fluids.

## Economic relevance
**Structurally controlled** ore deposits — fault/shear zones are key fluid pathways for
**gold** and base metals (see [Gold](../../03-mineral-resources/metallic/gold.md)).

## Identification notes
- Sheared/fine texture along faults; slickensides; ductile (mylonite) vs brittle
  (cataclasite) behaviour.

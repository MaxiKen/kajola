# Slate

## Composition
A very fine-grained, **low-grade** metamorphic rock formed from **shale/mudstone**;
composed of micas, chlorite, and quartz.

## Texture
Defined by **slaty cleavage** — it splits readily into thin, flat, smooth sheets.

## Diagnostic field features
- Excellent **slaty cleavage** (splits into thin flat sheets).
- Dull lustre; dark grey to black; very fine-grained.
- Lower grade than phyllite/schist.

## Host states / localities
Low-grade Basement areas — parts of the **schist belts** and adjoining metasedimentary
terrains (see [Siltstone, Shale & Mudstone](../sedimentary/siltstone-shale.md)).

## Associated minerals
Shale, phyllite, quartzite (grades upward into [Mica schist & Phyllite](mica-schist-phyllite.md)).

## Economic relevance
**Roofing slate**, tiles, and writing slates; a classic dimension material.

## Identification notes
- Splits into thin, flat, smooth sheets (slaty cleavage); dull and very fine — softer and
  duller than phyllite, which has a slight sheen.

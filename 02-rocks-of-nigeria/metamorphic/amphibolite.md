# Amphibolite

> **Family:** Metamorphic · **Origin:** metamorphosed basalt/gabbro (mafic rock)

## Composition
Chiefly **hornblende (amphibole)** + **plagioclase feldspar**, ± garnet, biotite,
quartz. Formed by metamorphism of mafic igneous rocks or marl.

## Texture
Typically **foliated to lineated** (aligned hornblende) but can be **massive**;
medium-to-coarse grained, dark green to black.

## Diagnostic field features
- **Dark green-black**, dense, dominated by **hornblende** (elongated black/green
  crystals).
- May show a **lineation** (minerals aligned in a direction).
- Harder than gabbro where well metamorphosed.

## Host states / localities
Occurs as bands and lenses within the **Basement Complex** and schist belts:
**Plateau, Kaduna, Oyo, Kwara, Kogi, Niger**, and elsewhere.

## Associated minerals
Garnet, plagioclase; sometimes **gold** where in the schist belts.

## Economic relevance
- **Aggregate and dimension stone**.
- Indicator of mafic rock in the sequence; occasionally gold-associated.

## Identification notes
- **Dark + hornblende-rich** = amphibolite.
- Distinguished from **gabbro** by foliation/lineation and hornblende (vs pyroxene).
- Hornblende shows the classic **~120° cleavage** under a hand lens.

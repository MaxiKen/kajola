# Granulite

## Composition
A **high-grade (granulite-facies)** metamorphic rock — quartz, feldspar, **pyroxene
(orthopyroxene)**, and garnet. **Charnockite** is the igneous (orthopyroxene-bearing
granitoid) member of this facies.

## Texture
Granoblastic, medium- to coarse-grained, massive (sometimes weakly gneissic).

## Diagnostic field features
- Pyroxene + garnet + quartz/feldspar; evidences **high-temperature** metamorphism.
- Darker, pyroxene-rich granulites; charnockite is the hypersthene-granite equivalent
  (see [Charnockite](../igneous/charnockite.md)).

## Host states / localities
High-grade Basement terranes — **Kaduna, Plateau, Bauchi**, and other north-central areas.

## Associated minerals
Charnockite, gneiss, migmatite, pyroxene, garnet (see [Banded gneiss](banded-gneiss.md),
[Migmatite](migmatite.md)).

## Economic relevance
Dimension stone; an indicator of deep crustal levels; can host mineralization.

## Identification notes
- Pyroxene + garnet + quartz/feldspar, high grade; closely related to (and transitional
  with) charnockite.

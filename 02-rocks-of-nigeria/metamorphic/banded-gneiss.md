# Banded Gneiss & Augen Gneiss

> **Family:** Metamorphic (medium-to-high grade) · **Composition:** granitic to dioritic

## Composition
Quartz, feldspar, mica and/or hornblende, ± **garnet, kyanite, sillimanite**.
Essentially a metamorphosed granite or sedimentary rock.

## Texture
Strongly **foliated and banded (gneissic)** — alternating light (quartz-feldspar)
and dark (mica/hornblende) mineral bands. **Augen gneiss** has large, lens- or
eye-shaped (**augen**) feldspar grains set in a finer foliated matrix.

## Diagnostic field features
- **Mineral banding** (light/dark layers), often wavy.
- **Augen** = big "eye" feldspar crystals in augen gneiss.
- Splits along foliation but less perfectly than schist.

## Host states / localities
Widespread in the **Basement Complex**: **Plateau, Kaduna, Oyo, Kwara, Niger,
Ekiti, Ondo, Kogi, Nasarawa**, and elsewhere.

## Associated minerals
Garnet, kyanite, sillimanite; **gold** in some gneissic belts; quartz and feldspar.

## Economic relevance
- **Dimension stone and aggregate**.
- Associated **gold** in places; source of **feldspar, quartz**.
- **Kyanite/sillimanite** (refractory minerals) occur locally.

## Identification notes
- **Banding/foliation** distinguishes gneiss from granite.
- **Augen eyes** = augen gneiss.
- Banding is **coarser and more irregular** than schist's fine foliation.

# 5.7 — Safety & Artisanal Small-Scale Mining (ASM)

Geology is a **field** discipline — and in Nigeria, safety and social context matter as
much as the science.

## Field safety
- **Heat & hydration:** carry plenty of water; work in the cooler hours; watch for
  heat exhaustion.
- **Health:** malaria prophylaxis; watch for snakes, scorpions, and stray animals.
- **Access:** tell someone your route; carry a charged phone and GPS; a first-aid kit.
- **PPE:** boots (snakes/thorns), gloves, helmet near pits, eye protection when hammering.
- **Mine visits:** never enter an unsupported pit alone; watch for **collapses, water,
  poor air, and blasting**.

## Artisanal & Small-Scale Mining (ASM)
ASM employs millions in Nigeria — **gold** (Osun, Zamfara, Kebbi…), **tin/columbite**
(Plateau, Bauchi), and others.

- **Risks:** unregulated pits (collapse), **mercury use** in gold processing, child
  labour, and conflict over land.
- **Your role as a professional:** promote **formalization, safe practice, and
  responsible sourcing**; support miners moving to **mercury-free** methods.
- **Respect communities:** engage local leaders; ASM sites can be sensitive.

## Environmental responsibility
- Minimize disturbance; **rehabilitate** pits and dumps; prevent water contamination.
- Properly dispose of chemicals (acid, mercury) — never into streams.
- Follow Nigerian regulations and the **Nigerian Minerals and Mining Act**.

## Quick safety checklist
- [ ] Water, food, first-aid, charged phone, GPS
- [ ] PPE (boots, gloves, helmet, glasses)
- [ ] Tell someone where you're going
- [ ] Never enter an unsafe pit; no lone working underground
- [ ] Report & flag hazards; engage the community

# 5.7 — Safety & Artisanal Small-Scale Mining (ASM)

> **What it is:** Geology is a **field** discipline — and in Nigeria, safety and social context matter as much as the science.
> **Golden rule:** Never take a risk to get a sample. *A sample is never worth an injury.*

Geology is a **field** discipline — and in Nigeria, safety and social context matter as much as the science.

## Field safety
- **Heat & hydration:** carry plenty of water; work in the cooler hours; watch for heat exhaustion.
- **Health:** malaria prophylaxis; watch for snakes, scorpions, and stray animals.
- **Access:** tell someone your route; carry a charged phone and GPS; a first-aid kit.
- **PPE:** boots (snakes/thorns), gloves, helmet near pits, eye protection when hammering.
- **Mine visits:** never enter an unsupported pit alone; watch for **collapses, water, poor air, and blasting**.

## Artisanal & Small-Scale Mining (ASM)
ASM employs millions in Nigeria — **gold** (Osun, Zamfara, Kebbi…), **tin/columbite** (Plateau, Bauchi), and others.
- **Risks:** unregulated pits (collapse), **mercury use** in gold processing, child labour, and conflict over land.
- **Your role as a professional:** promote **formalization, safe practice, and responsible sourcing**; support miners moving to **mercury-free** methods.
- **Respect communities:** engage local leaders; ASM sites can be sensitive.

## Environmental responsibility
- Minimize disturbance; **rehabilitate** pits and dumps; prevent water contamination.
- Properly dispose of chemicals (acid, mercury) — never into streams.
- Follow Nigerian regulations and the **Nigerian Minerals and Mining Act**.

## Radiation safety (uranium ground)
- Carry a **Geiger counter** in uranium ground (see [Uranium](../03-mineral-resources/metallic/uranium.md)).
- Avoid inhaling/ingesting dust; wash hands; do not eat/drink while sampling.
- Follow strict protocols and regulations for radioactive materials.

## Quick safety checklist
- [ ] Water, food, first-aid, charged phone, GPS
- [ ] PPE (boots, gloves, helmet, glasses)
- [ ] Tell someone where you're going
- [ ] Never enter an unsafe pit; no lone working underground
- [ ] Report & flag hazards; engage the community

## Common pitfalls
- **Working alone in a pit** — the leading cause of ASM fatalities.
- **Mercury exposure** in gold processing — promote mercury-free methods.
- **No PPE** when hammering or near pits.
- **Contaminating water** with acid or tailings.

## Facts
- ASM employs **millions** in Nigeria (gold, tin, columbite, and more).
- **Mercury** in gold processing is a major health and environmental hazard.
- The **Nigerian Minerals and Mining Act** governs safe, responsible mining.
- In uranium ground, a **Geiger counter** and dust precautions are essential.

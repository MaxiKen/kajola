# 5.6 — Worked Nigerian Examples

Tying the method to real Nigerian play types — *look → test → sample → interpret*.

## 1. Lode gold in the Ilesa schist belt (Osun)
- **Setting:** NE–SW mica schist/quartzite belt; quartz veins along shears.
- **Look for:** quartz veins, rusty **gossans**, pyritic/altered zones, ASM pits.
- **Test:** chip rock; quartz is hard & glassy; look for visible gold or sulfides.
- **Sample:** soil/rock geochem (**Au + As/Sb**) along the belt; pan streams.
- **Interpret:** the anomaly sits on the **shear zone**; follow it and its alteration halo.

## 2. Alluvial cassiterite on the Jos Plateau (Plateau)
- **Setting:** deeply weathered Younger Granite pegmatites; rivers rework the tin.
- **Look for:** heavy black/brown grains in stream gravels; old tin workings.
- **Test:** cassiterite is very hard & very heavy (heft it); resinous brown-black.
- **Sample:** pan; assay the **heavy-mineral fraction**; trench/auger the terrace gravels.
- **Interpret:** heavy mineral concentrates mark the river; trace upstream to pegmatite.

## 3. Lead-zinc at Abakaliki (Ebonyi/Enugu)
- **Setting:** Benue Trough sediments; stratabound/vein Pb-Zn.
- **Look for:** rusty **gossans**, calcite/quartz veins, old mines.
- **Test:** galena = very heavy, metallic, cubic cleavage; sphalerite = resinous yellow-brown.
- **Sample:** map & channel-sample veins/gossans; assay Pb, Zn, Ag.
- **Interpret:** rusty porous caps + dense cubic galena = a strong target.

## 4. Ironstone at Itakpe (Kogi)
- **Setting:** Banded Iron Formation, often lateritized/enriched.
- **Look for:** hard red-brown hematitic ridges/canga.
- **Test:** heavy, red streak; magnetic if magnetite-rich.
- **Sample:** trenches/drill across the band; assay Fe + impurities (Si, Al, P).
- **Interpret:** thick, high-grade, low-impurity ironstone = an ore body.

## 5. Bitumen at Ondo (Dahomey Basin)
- **Setting:** tar-sand horizons in Cretaceous sands.
- **Look for:** black, sticky, oil-smelling sands; seeps.
- **Test:** stains hands black; floats/softens with heat.
- **Sample:** drill & assay bitumen content by Soxhlet/assay.
- **Interpret:** thick, high-saturation tar sand = resource (see [Bitumen](../03-mineral-resources/energy/bitumen.md)).

## 6. Limestone for cement at Ewekoro (Ogun)
- **Setting:** Ewekoro Formation carbonate.
- **Look for:** light grey, hard, bedded carbonate.
- **Test:** **fizzes** in dilute HCl (calcite); high CaO, low Mg.
- **Sample:** trench/channel across the bed; assay CaO, MgO, SiO₂.
- **Interpret:** thick, low-impurity limestone = cement feed (see [Limestone](../03-mineral-resources/industrial/limestone.md)).

## The pattern
Every example follows the same logic: **know the play → recognize the surface clue →
confirm with a field test → sample systematically → interpret against geology.**
